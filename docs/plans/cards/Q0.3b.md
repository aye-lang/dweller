# Q0.3b: a Target column beside the floors

- goal (decision 3 of `foundations.md`, its default): `docs/conformance.md` keeps "Threshold %" as the floor that `conformance.py check` enforces, and gains a "Target %" for the X2 areas and the Q6 areas, each with one line that says where it comes from.
- source of the targets: "the score of the subtests that do not need a deferred feature" (the row's second source). The first source, what the 20 sites and the daily sites use, needs the site archives, which are on the owner's Mac.
- rule: target = 90 % of (the area's subtests that need no deferred feature) over (all its subtests), in whole points, never under the floor. A deferred feature is a path pattern with a reason in `browser/tools/conformance/targets.json`: a feature in no stable browser or in one engine only, and WPT's `tentative` tests everywhere. The 90 % is a proposal (no engine passes every subtest of these directories); it is one number in the file (`share`).
- design:
  - `browser/tools/conformance/targets.json`: `share`, the patterns that hold everywhere, and per area its deferred patterns with reasons and the counted numbers (`target`, `counted`, `total`).
  - `browser/tools/conformance_targets.py`, `conformance.py targets`: counts the patterns in each area's committed baseline (in this machine's results where an area has no baseline file), writes the numbers back, and refreshes the column and the "Targets" section of the document, touching nothing else in it. `conformance.py table` adds the same column and section to a new table.
  - `conformance.py check` is unchanged by this card: it does not read a target.
  - `scoreboard.py summary` (DV6) says how many areas with a target are at it.
- files: `browser/tools/conformance_targets.py`, `browser/tools/conformance/targets.json`, `browser/tools/conformance.py`, `browser/tools/scoreboard.py`, `docs/conformance.md` (the Target column and the "Targets" section under the table), `docs/plans/cards/Q0.3b.md`
