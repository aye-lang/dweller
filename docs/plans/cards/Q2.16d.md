# Q2.16d: a child frame's renderer with nothing in view is throttled

- goal: with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site`, an out-of-process child frame of which no pixel is in the tab's viewport (scrolled away, or clipped out) runs as a background tab's renderer does: its timers at most once a second. Before, it ran at full rate for as long as its tab was shown.
- source: the readiness list of Q2, "Speed with the flag", last bullet (Q2.9 left; process reuse, the bullet's other half, stays with Q2.13).
- design:
  - no new message: the wave's `visible{on}` (83, Q2.14: a hidden renderer's `runTimers` runs once a second, `backgroundWaits`) and Q2.10's `viewport_intersection` (the child's visible rect, `TabProcess.seen`).
  - browser: `frameSeen(child)` is whether the child's visible rect is not empty (true until the first rect was told). `tellIntersections` tells the child `visible` when its rect changes: on when its embedder is visible and the rect is not empty, off else. `tellVisible`'s walk down the children (a tab that goes to the background or comes back) uses the same rule, so a child out of view stays off when its tab is shown again, and a child's children follow their embedder.
  - the renderer side is the wave's: a hidden renderer still paints a frame that is due, so the frame is current when it scrolls into view, and `visible{on}` makes one due.
- Chromium read: `third_party/blink/renderer/core/frame/remote_frame_view.cc` (`UpdateRenderThrottlingStatus`: a frame hidden or with an empty viewport intersection is render-throttled), `third_party/blink/renderer/platform/scheduler/common/throttling/budget_pool.cc` (the budget of a throttled frame's timers). Dweller takes: no visible rect means the background rate.
- files: new `browser/ui/frame-seen.aye`, `browser/ui/{tell-visible,tell-intersections}.aye`; new `browser/tests/ui/{oopif-throttle,oopif-throttle-child}.html`, `tests/browser/window_renderer_oopif_throttle.rs` (and its `mod` pair).
- verify (2026-11-17, Linux x86_64, 4 cores; proxy variables unset for the tests):
  - `aye check browser`: exit 0. `aye test browser`: 314 passed, 0 failed. `check_taste.py`, `check_layers.py`, `gen_property_ids.py --check`: exit 0. `browser/build.sh`: exit 0.
  - `cargo test --release --test browser`: 275 passed, 0 failed (296 s; 295 passed after the merge of the wave branch that followed), with `window_renderer_oopif_input_intersection` (a child scrolled into view gets its observer's report) and the `window_renderer_oopif*` families.
  - new `window_renderer_oopif_throttle_hidden` (`ui/oopif-throttle.html`: a `localhost` frame whose page counts a 20 ms timer in its title, over 300 window frames): 123 ticks with the frame in view, 3 with it 2000 px below the viewport. The binary without this card counts 127 out of view.
  - No golden re-blessed; every window headless.
