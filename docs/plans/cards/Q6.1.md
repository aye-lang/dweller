- goal: the area (`css/css-backgrounds`, `css/css-borders`) reaches its threshold of 65 (30.1 at the start). One cause per cluster, largest first.
- design (clusters in the order done; subtests gained are of the area's 8473):
  - `border-image` did not exist. Five longhands appended to `PROPERTIES` (ids 179 to 183: `BORDER_IMAGE_SOURCE/SLICE/WIDTH/OUTSET/REPEAT`, `PKind.Layers`, kept as canonical text) and the `border-image` shorthand (`border-image-shorthand.aye`, `split-border-image.aye`). Grammar and serialization in `write-border-image-*.aye`; computed quads collapse like `CSSQuadValue` (`write-quad.aye`). Painting is not done (see Left).
  - Text values now transition (`mixes-text.aye`: `PKind.Layers` as well as transform parts) and `allow-discrete` flips at 50% (`allows-discrete.aye`, `value-at.aye`).
  - Web Animations: `composite` add/accumulate on keyframes and effects, CSS `animation-composition` (`animation-values.js`, `css-animations.js`, `animation/add-text.aye`, native `ak.add`); repeatable lists and quads are matched before mixing (`match-values.aye`); shorthands in keyframes expand to longhands (native `ak.expand`).
  - `box-shadow`: validated, stored as `color x y blur spread inset` (`box-shadow-value.aye`), computed form `rgb(...) 1px 2px 0px 0px` (`write-computed-shadows.aye`, `write-shadow.aye`), shadow-list interpolation and add (`mix-shadows.aye`, `add-shadows.aye`).
  - CSSOM setter: invalid values of `background-*` layers, `border-*-radius`, `box-shadow`, `border-image-*` and the `background`, `border*` shorthands are rejected (`valid-layers.aye`, `valid-position.aye`, `specified-shorthand.aye`, `checks-value.aye`). Side shorthands `border-top-radius` etc. (`border-side-radius.aye`).
  - `style.borderTopColor` and the like read from a shorthand declaration in the style attribute (`shorthand-part.aye`, `border-part.aye`, `engine/style-shorthand-value.aye`). `getComputedStyle` answers `margin`, `padding`, `border-width/style/color` and `border-radius` (`write-computed-shorthand.aye`).
- references: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-backgrounds-3/ , https://drafts.csswg.org/web-animations-1/#effect-composition . Chromium read for algorithms: `core/css/properties/longhands/` (border-image longhands: quad serialization, `fill` position), `core/animation/` (composite, repeatable list); Dweller takes the serialization rules and list matching only.
