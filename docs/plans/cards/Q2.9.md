# Q2.9: out-of-process iframes 1 (placeholder, child renderer, composite)

- goal: with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site` (new; it is `tab` plus out-of-process iframes) a cross-site iframe runs in a renderer of its own. The parent's renderer lays the iframe out as a remote-frame placeholder (it keeps the box), reports where its content box is, and the window composites the child's frame there, at the parent's scroll and clip, from the child's shared-memory buffers as for a tab. The child's own navigations stay in it; a resized iframe resizes the child; a crashed child shows a sad frame in its rect only; a removed iframe ends its renderer. Same-site iframes stay in the parent's renderer. Without the flag (and with `tab`) nothing changes.
- design:
  - parent's engine (`engine/remote-frame.aye`, `remote-page.aye`): `setSiteFrames(on)` (the renderer's `--site-frames` spawn argument). `remoteFrame(p, target)`: an http(s) URL whose scheme or site (`net.sameSite`, eTLD+1) is not the root page's. `framedDocument`, `navigateFrame` (a parent's link or script into the frame) and the background prefetch `startFrameLoads` then never fetch it: `remotePage` makes an empty document at the frame's URL with `Page.remote = true`, and `placeFrame` gives it an opaque origin and no scripts (`SB_ORIGIN | SB_SCRIPTS`). It lays out and paints as an empty frame (its canvas color, white), so the parent's own pixels in the content box are the white the child covers.
  - IPC (type code 43, after `OutOfMemory` 42): R→B `subframes{frames}`, one record per remote frame of the renderer's local frames (`ipc.Subframe`: `key` = the placeholder's page id, `x y` the content box's origin in the parent's buffer in device px, possibly negative, `width height` its device size, `clip` `x0 y0 x1 y1` = the box ∩ the viewport ∩ the content boxes of the local frames above it, `url`). The renderer (`reportSubframes`, `putRemoteFrames`, from `paintFrame` after layout) sends it only when the bytes change, before the `frame` of the same layout. A child renderer gets the existing `navigate`, `resize` and `frame_ack` on a frame id of its own (from 0x40000001 up); no other new message.
  - browser (`ui/`): `TabProcess.children: List(ChildFrame)` (key, child process, rect, clip, `src`) and `TabProcess.embedder`. `takeSubframes` checks and applies the list: removed keys end their renderer (`endChild` → `endTabProcess`, graceful shutdown), a new key gets a `ChildFrame`, a moved, resized or reclipped one updates it; any change repaints the parent's area. `serviceChildren` (each poll, recursive, so a child's own cross-site frames nest): starts the child (`startChild` → `startTabProcess`, so the child is sandboxed and in the storage-event list), gives it buffers of the frame's size (`giveBuffers`, now by size and `tp.tab`), navigates it once it said `hello` (`navigateChild`, the parent's URL as referrer), services its socket (`serviceChild`, `takeChildMessages` → `childSent`), and swaps it (`swapChild`) when its navigation's response is of another site than its lock (`takesSwap`, Q2.6b): a new renderer locked to the new site gets the kept response (`primed`), the old one is killed. A child's `begin_navigation` (script, form) navigates the child itself.
  - the child's requests: `contextTop` is the tab's top, so the child's document and subresource requests and `document.cookie` are third-party, as for an in-process cross-site frame (no cookies); its claimed `top` is still checked against its own committed URL, which is the root of its renderer. Storage is its own origin's (the child is locked to its site, so Q2.7's "no storage for a cross-site frame in its parent's renderer" does not apply to it).
  - composite (`addTabDamage` → `addChildDamage`, `copyTabFrame` → `copyShown` + `copyChildFrames`): after the parent's pixels of a damaged rect, each live child's shown buffer is copied into that rect ∩ its clip, at its offset, recursively; a child's damage rects are moved to its offset and clipped. A gone child fills its clip with the sad-frame gray (232, 234, 237). Until a child's frame of the new size comes the parent's white shows.
  - frame-ancestors / X-Frame-Options (Q3.6): in this mode the framed document never reaches the parent's engine, so `placeChecked` cannot run. The browser checks the child's navigation response (`framingRefused`, in `answerFetch`): CSP `frame-ancestors` (it overrides XFO) and XFO against every embedder process's committed URL; a refusal answers the child's fetch with `AccessDenied`, so the child commits its error page, the refused bytes reach no renderer, and the child gets no site lock.
  - test hooks: `--events` words `renderer:child` (every child of the shown tab has shown a frame of its frame's size), `renderer:kill-child` (SIGKILL to the first child), `renderer:child-gone`; `--state` lines `renderer-child PID SITE frame W H at X Y title TITLE` or `renderer-child PID SITE gone REASON`; `--until-idle` waits for the children too (`rendererSettled`). `DWELLER_TEST_EVIL=subframes` (with `DWELLER_TEST=1`): the parent names its own URL as a remote frame.
- the browser's checks of `subframes` (kill `bad subframes`): well formed (records read to the end), at most 16, keys non-zero and unique, `x y` integral and within ±2^24, size 1..16384, the clip ordered, within 16384 and inside the frame's rect (or empty), the URL 1..2 MiB, an http(s) URL of another site than the sender's lock (a renderer never names a same-site frame), and a key's URL never changes. A child renderer's messages (`childSent`) are checked as a tab's: on its frame id; `loaded` against the URL navigated to and its lock (`commit outside site lock`); `same_document` same origin; `redirect` once per navigation; `fetch`, `cookie_*`, `storage` through the Q2.4/Q2.7 checks with its own lock; `begin_navigation` through `initiatorMayLoad`; `agent_reply` and `input_ack` (never asked of a child) kill (`unexpected in a frame`).
- Chromium read: `third_party/blink/renderer/core/frame/remote_frame.cc` (`FrameRectsChanged`, `SynchronizeVisualProperties`: the parent's layout sends the remote frame's rect and size), `third_party/blink/renderer/core/frame/remote_frame_view.cc` (`PropagateFrameRectsInternal`: the content box in the local root, pixel-snapped; `Paint` records only a foreign layer, no pixels), `content/browser/renderer_host/render_frame_proxy_host.cc` (`SynchronizeVisualProperties` to the child's connector, `Detach` removes a remote child, `ChildProcessGone` shows the sad frame in the embedder), `content/browser/renderer_host/frame_tree_node.cc` (the browser's tree of frames, each with its current host), `components/viz/service/display/surface_aggregator.cc` (idea only: `EmitSurfaceContent` draws a child surface at its quad, clipped; `TransformRectToDestRootTargetSpace` moves a child's damage to the root and clips it; a missing surface gets a default color). Dweller takes: the parent reports pixel-snapped content boxes and paints nothing of the child; the browser owns the child processes and composites translation and clip only.
- files: new `browser/ipc/{subframe,put-subframe,read-subframe,bad-subframe,subframe_test}.aye`; `browser/ipc/{message,decode,put-payload,from-renderer}.aye`; new `browser/engine/{remote-frame,remote-page}.aye`, `browser/engine/{engine,frames}.aye`; new `browser/renderer/{report-subframes,put-remote-frames}.aye`, `browser/renderer/{serve,serve-on,tab-renderer,paint-frame}.aye`; new `browser/ui/{child-frame,take-subframes,place-child,child-cross-site,drop-removed-children,end-child,start-child,navigate-child,service-children,service-child,take-child-messages,child-sent,swap-child,context-top,framing-refused,child-damaged,add-child-damage,copy-shown,copy-child-frames,fill-sad-frame,child-waits,kill-renderer,write-child-processes}.aye`, `browser/ui/{tab-process,start-tab-process,end-tab-process,poll-tab-processes,give-buffers,show-tab-frame,service-speculative,renderer-sent,answer-fetch,doc-cookies,add-tab-damage,copy-tab-frame,write-tab-process,renderer-waits,renderer-settled,tab-process-timeout,script,window}.aye`, `browser/main.aye` (`dweller agent` relays with `site` as with `tab`); new `browser/tests/ui/{oopif,oopif-child}.html`; new `tests/browser/window_renderer_oopif.rs` (and its `mod` pair in `tests/browser.rs`); `docs/process-model.md` (the frames row).

## Status: done, 2026-11-09

Left:
- input to the child (Q2.10): clicks, keys and wheel over the frame go to the parent's renderer, which sees an empty placeholder; the child's cursor, status URL, focus and long-task reports are checked and dropped.
- window proxies (Q2.11): the parent's `iframe.contentWindow` is the placeholder's opaque, scriptless window (`location` reads the frame's first URL, never its later ones); `postMessage`, `frames`, names, `parent`/`top` from the child are not wired. The iframe's `load` event fires when the placeholder loads, not when the child's document does.
- process reuse (process-model §3 rules 1 and 2): every remote frame gets a new renderer; a frame of the parent's own site reached by the child's navigation (`?away`), or a grandchild of the top's site, runs in a renderer of its own instead of the parent's. Spare processes and the process cap are Q2.13.
- compositing: translation and clip only; the clip does not include `overflow` clips, transforms, opacity or content painted above the iframe (process-model §5 "over" buffer); a child frame's pixels are not damage-checked by `DWELLER_DAMAGE_CHECK`.
- the frame-ancestors / XFO check sees only the embedder processes' top documents, not same-site in-process frames between the top and the remote frame (they can be of other origins of the same site); a refused frame shows the network error page ("Cannot load page") instead of "This content is blocked.", and the parent's console gets no line.
- frame navigations in the browser's history: a child's navigations add no session-history entry, and `history.back()` in a child is dropped; a POST into a remote frame by the parent's link or form target loads it by GET; a child's cross-site navigation refetches nothing (the kept response is used) but loses its referrer (the parent's URL is sent).
- the decision is made on the URL before redirects: an iframe that redirects back to the parent's site still runs in its own renderer, locked to the final site.
- a child renderer's `sessionStorage` is keyed by its frame id, not by the tab (and is not dropped when the tab closes); hang detection (`ping`) is not run for children.
- the window wakes for a child's frames at the tab's poll rate (no IO thread yet, as for tabs).
