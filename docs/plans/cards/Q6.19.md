- cause fixed (cluster 1). css-transforms-2 §14 interpolates `perspective()` through the matrix; §13.1 decomposes and recomposes a matrix with its perspective row.
  - `parseTransformFn` reads `perspective(none)` (no argument) and `writeTransformFn` writes it back; `identityTransform` of `perspective()` is `perspective(none)`.
  - `transformMatrix`: `perspective(none)` is the identity, a depth below 1px is used as 1px (`perspectiveInverse`).
  - `mixPerspectives` (chromium `perspective_transform_operation.cc` `Blend`): a pair mixes the inverse of the used depths; a result of 0 or below is `perspective(none)`.
  - `decompose` divides by `m[15]` and solves the perspective row (`perspectiveOf`, the spec's `inverse(perspectiveMatrix)` step written with cross products, since the matrix without its perspective row is affine); `mixDecomposed` mixes the four components; `recompose` starts from the perspective matrix times the translation, as the spec's pseudo code does. Without a perspective row every result is the same as before (the extra product is with the identity).
