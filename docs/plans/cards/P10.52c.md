# P10.52c: line-clamp and -webkit-line-clamp

- goal: real sites clamp card titles and previews with `display: -webkit-box; -webkit-box-orient: vertical; -webkit-line-clamp: N; overflow: hidden`; css-overflow-4 adds the standard `line-clamp`. Clamp after N lines, the box ends at those lines, the last one gets the ellipsis; WPT `css/overflow` (46.8%, threshold 60%) toward its threshold.
- design: new longhands `max-lines` (`auto || <integer [1,∞]>`, computed as text, integer first), `block-ellipsis` (`no-ellipsis | ellipsis | <string>`, inherited), `continue` (`normal | discard | collapse | -webkit-legacy`). `line-clamp` (`none | [<integer> || auto || <'block-ellipsis'>] -webkit-legacy?`) and `-webkit-line-clamp` (`none | <integer>`, block-ellipsis `ellipsis`, continue `-webkit-legacy`) are now shorthands of the three, as the spec defines them; both serialize from the longhands (`getComputedStyle`, inline style), and setting either in inline style drops the longhand declarations. Layout: a block container clamps when `continue` is `collapse`, or `-webkit-legacy` on a `-webkit-box`/`-webkit-inline-box` with `-webkit-box-orient: vertical`, and `max-lines` has an integer: the box's height ends after that line of its block flow (nested blocks counted as before), the line gets `block-ellipsis` (a string replaces `…`; `no-ellipsis` none) when content follows. With `collapse` the lines after are not laid out (not painted, not hit-testable); with `-webkit-legacy` they stay as overflow (Chrome; `overflow: hidden` clips them). `display: -webkit-box` with orient vertical and a non-`normal` `continue` reads back as `flow-root` (`-webkit-inline-box`: `inline-block`) in `getComputedStyle` (Chrome's rule, `webkit-box-computed.html`); layout of `-webkit-box` is unchanged. `CSS.supports(text)` retries `text` in parentheses (css-conditional-4), so `CSS.supports("line-clamp: 2")` holds.
