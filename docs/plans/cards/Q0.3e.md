# Q0.3e: a capture makes a script's requests as its replay does, so bbc's archive replays

- goal: bbc's recorded archive replays to the end. The replay missed 8 requests (`securepubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/ads` x5, `ping.chartbeat.net/ping`, `comcluster.cxense.com/Repo/rep.gif`, `elb.the-ozone-project.com/static/load-cookie.html`), whose URLs held other values than the recorded ones. bbc was the last W2 site that failed (19 of 20 pass).

## Cause

The capture and the replay seed the same sources the same way (`DWELLER_BENCHMARK=1` in both: `Date` is the epoch plus the virtual clock, `Math.random` and `ak.randomFill` are LCGs). They ran other tasks:

1. A script's request ran in the background while recording (`net.liveRequest` true, `net.startRequest`). Since fetch and XHR stream (Q5.8, 2026-11-19), its body comes in the parts the network sends, and each part is a task 10 ms later on the virtual clock (`engine/stream-turn.aye`, `part-task.aye`). The replay has `liveRequest` false: `net.request` answers from the archive at once, whole, in one task. So the capture's clock ran 10 ms ahead for each extra part, and the tasks between drew `Math.random()` in another order.
   - Evidence: a live record of bbc with a log in `takeStreamPart` took 5 parts, 4 of them only the head: `elb.the-ozone-project.com/openrtb2/auction` (twice), `ep1.adtrafficquality.google/getconfig/sodar`, `elb.the-ozone-project.com/cookie_sync`, and `pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle.js` (2363 bytes, then the rest). Replaying that archive at once, the first `gampad/ads` differed only in `correlator` and `dt` (578 against 568), then 5 more misses followed from it. Q0.3d's guess, "network answers that come at other times live", was near: it is the number of parts, not the order of the answers.
2. A request in the background is recorded when it ends (`endRequest`). The khaos.json XHR was sent just before the dump ended and was dropped unrecorded; the replay asks the archive for it when it is sent.

## Fix

- `net.liveRequest` (`browser/net/loader.aye`) is false under `DWELLER_RECORD` as under `DWELLER_REPLAY`: a recording makes a script's request (fetch, XHR, `EventSource`, an inserted script, a module, a frame) as the replay does, at once and whole, so the page gets the same tasks in both. Written in `browser/engine/PERF.md` ("Archive semantics") and the practice "Record a request the way the replay answers it".
- Q0.3d's record of the parts a page took (`Ask.taken`, `browser/net/archive-streamed.aye`) has no use left (no request streams while recording) and is removed; its test `archive_keeps_streamed_bodies` and fixture `archive_streamed.html` are replaced by the test below.
- No URL rewriting and no ignore list in the matcher: every value became equal.
- Cost: a capture makes these requests one after the other. bbc's live dump took 46 to 58 s before, 71 s after; `capture --site bbc` 1:42.
