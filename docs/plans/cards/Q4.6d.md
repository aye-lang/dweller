What each is:

- `relayout:not_element` is not a climb that fails. A restyle that moves more than 64 elements (`MAX_MOVED`: values that layout reads changed) keeps no list (`Styles.moved_many`), and the dirty list becomes the document node, which `relayoutDirty` refuses at once. All 30 are this.
- `relayout:shadows` (`aboveShadows`, P4.75v): a relayout root inside a shadow tree or under a host was refused, though boxes are built from the flat tree in both paths.
- `relayout:dropped` on `perf/animated.html`: see "Not fixed".

## Fix

- moved-many (`Styles.moved_more`, `keepMoved`; `movedTop`, `commonAncestor`, `flatDepth`, `subtreeUnder` in the engine; Blink `LayoutObject::MarkContainerChainForLayout` marks the chain of each changed box, here one root for them all): the moved elements beyond the first 64 are kept (4096 at most), and the dirty element is their common ancestor in the flat tree, beside the other dirty elements. Not when that ancestor is the root element or `body`, or holds more than half of the document's elements: measured on `class_sets.html`, a relayout of a subtree that is most of the page costs about twice a full layout (3.0 to 3.5 ms against 1.4 ms: its boxes are built again and the old ones stay in the list), so those keep the full layout.
- shadow trees: `aboveShadows` is gone; the climb from a dirty element to a box that can be built again goes by the flat tree (`dom.flatParent`: from a shadow tree's top to its host, from a slotted child to its slot), so a root inside a shadow tree, or a slotted child, is built and laid out again like any block.
- files: new `browser/css/keep-moved.aye`; `browser/engine/moved-top.aye`, `common-ancestor.aye`, `flat-depth.aye`, `subtree-under.aye`; removed `engine/above-shadows.aye`; `css/cascade.aye` (`Styles.moved_more`, 5 lines), `engine/geometry.aye` (2 dirty lists, the climb, the shadow check: 8 lines). Nothing in `browser/layout/`. Fixture `browser/tests/jsdom/relayout_moved.html` (`.tree`), test `jsdom_relayout_moved_part_of_page`.

## Not fixed

- `perf/animated.html`, `relayout:dropped` 52 of 158 frames: one animated cell is an inline `span` among 1000 in `#grid`, and the nearest box that can be built again is `#grid`, the whole page. Each frame builds all its boxes again (10.2 ms per relayout that succeeds), and every third frame the box list is over `2 × built + 1024` and a full layout (8.1 ms) cleans it. The exit costs nothing: the full layout is the cheaper of the two. What costs is the root: a changed inline box lays out its whole block container, and that container's boxes are built again though only one changed. Blink lays out the line's formatting context and reuses the inline items (`core/layout/inline/inline_node.cc`, `inline_cursor`); here that is work in `browser/layout/` (`rebuild`, `canRebuild`), shared with css-layout and css-writing.
- A relayout of a large subtree costs about twice a full layout of the same boxes (above). Until it does not, any root that holds most of the page is better refused.
- `local:unnoted` 3: sheet changes on pages that hit `sheet:kept` (Q4.5).
