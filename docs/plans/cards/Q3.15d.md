Slice 1 (2026-11-22, one cause, 2409 -> 2688 of 4128; the area has 4128 subtests once `SharedWorker` files run):
- `SharedWorker` did not exist. It runs on the dedicated worker's thread and link (`engine/workers/worker-page.js`, `worker-scope.js`): the worker's global is a `SharedWorkerGlobalScope` with `name` and `onconnect` and no `postMessage`; each `new SharedWorker` is a connection, a port numbered in the envelope of its messages (`connect` carries a `MessagePort`-like port, whose messages wait until `start()` or `onmessage`); within a document a second `new SharedWorker` of the same URL, name and type connects to the running one. The script is checked as a dedicated worker's (`worker-src`, mixed content) and a refused one fires `error` at the object. Native: `Link.shared` and `shared_name` (`workers/make-shared.aye`), `wkStart`'s fourth argument, the scope's `shared` and `name`.
- Chromium read: `content/browser/worker_host/shared_worker_service_impl.cc` (one worker per (URL, name, storage key), connections as ports), `third_party/blink/renderer/core/workers/shared_worker_global_scope.cc`.
- verify: `wpt.py run content-security-policy` 2688/4128 (65.1%); `aye test browser` 364; `cargo test --release --test browser` (site mode) 379 / 379.

Slice 2 (2026-11-22, seven causes, 2688 -> 3320 of 4128):
- CSP Embedded Enforcement (`embedded-enforcement` 147 -> 286 of 290). An iframe's `csp` attribute (reflected) is its required policy when it is valid (one policy, no `report-uri`/`report-to`, `csp.validRequiredPolicy`) and its parent document's required policy subsumes it; else the parent's is (`engine/required-csp.aye`). Frame navigations send it as `Sec-Required-CSP` (`fetch-frame-page.aye`; such frames skip the early load). The response may embed if its scheme is local, its `Allow-CSP-From` is `*` or the embedder's origin, or its own policies subsume the required one (`embedding-refusal.aye`, `blanket-enforced.aye`); else the frame shows the error page of `frame-ancestors`. When the response's policies do not subsume it, the required policy is enforced on the document (`keep-required-csp.aye`). The intersection of policies (3.1) and subsumption (3.2) of the CSPEE draft are in `csp/` (`policy-subsumes.aye` and its parts, unit test `policy-subsumes_test.aye`), with what Chromium's tests ask past the draft: an empty list is 'none', a `default-src` of the required policy is compared as each fetch directive it stands for, `'strict-dynamic'` voids host, scheme and 'self' sources, `'unsafe-inline'` is void beside a nonce or hash in `style-src` too, `*` stands for no `data:` or `blob:`, `plugin-types` and unknown directives count for nothing.
- Worklets (`gen/` +340 with the next cause): `CSS.paintWorklet` and `BaseAudioContext.audioWorklet` (as Chromium ships; no animation or layout worklet) are `Worklet`s whose `addModule` runs the module in a worker thread with a `PaintWorkletGlobalScope` (`registerPaint`) or `AudioWorkletGlobalScope` (`registerProcessor`), without `postMessage`, timers or fetch (`worker-scope.js`). The module and its imports are checked by the page's `script-src-elem` (`wk-worklet-refused.aye`, `worker-load-refused.aye`), fetched in CORS mode, and `addModule` resolves once the graph loaded and ran (`READY`, kind 10, `evaluate-worklet.aye`); a module that does not load or parse rejects it with `AbortError`, one that throws when it runs does not. Each `addModule` has a global scope of its own.
- The imports of a module worker's script were not checked by the page's `worker-src` (`gen/*worker-import`: +112), nor the URL a worker's script landed on after redirects (`wk-fetch.aye`, `worker-refused.aye`).
- `nonce` (`nonce-hiding` 17 -> 93 of 97): `HTMLElement`, `SVGElement` and `MathMLElement` have the `nonce` IDL attribute over the element's cryptographic nonce (`dom.nonceOf`, `dom.setNonce`), copied by `cloneNode`; when a CSP header came with the document, an element's `nonce` attribute is emptied once it is connected (`dom/hide-nonces.aye`, at load and at each insertion), so selectors cannot read it, and CSP checks the kept nonce (`element-nonce.aye`).
- `report-uri` (`reporting` 17 -> 38 of 61): a violation POSTs a `csp-report` (`application/csp-report`) to each `report-uri` of its policy, once per distinct violation of a document (the start of the refused code tells violations apart), as a request the page's CSP does not check (`report-destination.aye`); `blockedURI` loses its user, password and fragment. WPT's `reporting/resources` is now fetched with the checkout (`wpt.py`'s `EXTRA_DIRS`).
- Timers and eval (`unsafe-eval` 10 -> 18 of 37): `setTimeout` and `setInterval` with a string the page's policy does not let compile report it and return 0, and a string they took runs later even when a policy came meanwhile (`ak-string-code-refused.aye`); a report-only policy reports `eval` and the rest without blocking them (`page-eval-refusal.aye`).
- `crypto.subtle.digest` (SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512, `ak-digest.aye`): `script-src/tentative` hashes URLs with it.
- Also: a worker's `fetch` and `XMLHttpRequest` are CORS requests of the page (kind 9, `scope-connect.aye`), where they were no-cors script loads that ORB refused across origins; `net/psl.aye` no longer panics on a host that ends in a dot (`generic/src-trailing-dot` crashed).
- verify: `wpt.py run content-security-policy` 3320/4128 (80.4%; baseline written); `aye test browser --module csp` 13, `--module net` 51.
