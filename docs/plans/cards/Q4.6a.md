# Q4.6a: relayout next to floats from the formatting root that holds them

- goal: a change in a page with floats is laid out again from the nearest block formatting context root that holds the floats around it, not as a full layout of the document. The `relayout:floats` refusal of `relayoutDirty` goes away.
- cause: Q4.1 (PR #264): `relayoutDirty` refused every tree with a float (`g.floats`). zol refused 1378 times in one scroll run: a scroll handler reads geometry 274–338 times per frame and each read was a full layout of 17 ms (p95 frame 4680 ms of full layout). mistral idled with 94 `relayout:floats` refusals.
- design:
  - floats never leave a block formatting context root (`isFormattingRoot`: the root, `flow-root`, `overflow` other than visible, out-of-flow boxes, inline-blocks, flex, grid and table children). A root's content is laid out from `fx = fy = 0` with its own float list, so it can be laid out again alone with the containing block it had.
  - `holdsFloats(t, i)` (layout/holds-floats.aye): is there a float in the formatting context of box `i`: in its children, and under those that are not roots (inline boxes always looked into).
  - `relayoutFrom(t, i)` (layout/relayout-from.aye): the box to lay out again for box `i`: `i` itself, or the nearest formatting root at or above it when that root holds floats.
  - `relayoutUp` (layout/incremental.aye) starts at `relayoutFrom(i)` and, when the box comes out with another size, goes on at `relayoutFrom(parent)`: a root that grows goes up to the next root that holds floats, as Blink's `MarkContainerChainForLayout` goes up to a relayout boundary. A box whose formatting context holds floats keeps none of its children's layouts (`Tree.keep_under` off): a kept child would not put its floats in `Tree.floats`, nor see moved ones.
  - `rebuild` no longer refuses the floats it builds; `relayoutDirty` no longer refuses trees with floats. `Geometry.floats`, `Boxes.floats` and `hasFloats` are gone.
  - the root's position next to the floats outside it (`flow`: the band at its top, 1 px high) does not depend on its own height, so a root laid out again with the same size stays where it is.
- files: `browser/layout/holds-floats.aye`, `browser/layout/relayout-from.aye` (new), `browser/layout/incremental.aye`, `browser/engine/geometry.aye`, `browser/tests/ui/relayout_floats.html` (new), `tests/browser.rs` (`window_relayout_floats`), `browser/engine/PERF.md`, `docs/plans/cards/Q4.6a.md` (new)
- verify: `window_relayout_floats` (a text beside a float in a flow-root, a text that the float of the block before wraps around, a flow-root that grows next to a float in another root): no full layout after the first frame, every relayout equal to a full one (`DWELLER_LAYOUT_CHECK`); with `relayoutFrom` returning `i` (no lifting) the check finds 24 differences. The `layout`, `window_` and `dump` tests in one capped run; zol and mistral W7 and `DWELLER_LAYOUT_WHY` before and after; WPT `css/floats-tables`; `$AYE test browser`, `$AYE check browser`, `check_taste.py`.
