# Q2.15i: a child frame's history entries

- goal: second item of the lane's second list [Q2.9 Left: "a child's navigations add no session-history entry, and `history.back()` in a child is dropped"]. With `DWELLER_PROCESS=site`, a navigation a cross-site frame makes of itself is an entry of its tab's history, and Back and Forward across it move the frame, not the page around it.
- design (browser only, no new message):
  - `childSent`: a child's `begin_navigation` that the browser follows marks the child (`self_nav`) and keeps the URL it leaves (`before`); its `loaded` (not after a failed load) turns that into `pushes`. A renderer swap of the child carries both (`swapChild`). The frame's first load and a navigation the browser itself asks for (a traversal) are not marked.
  - `noteFrameStep` (from `serviceChildren`, which has the window): the tab's history gets a new entry that repeats the current one (URL, document number, state), and the tab's top process records a `FrameStep` {entry index, document number, the child's key, URL before, URL after}. Chromium does the same: a subframe navigation adds an entry cloned from the last one with that frame's item changed.
  - `goToEntry`, for a traversal inside one top document: `traverseFrames` walks from the current entry to the target; each crossing that is a frame step navigates that child (`childByKey`, `navigateChild`) to the step's URL after (forward) or before (back). When every crossing was a frame step, nothing is sent to the top renderer; when some were the top's own entries (fragments, `pushState`), `traverse` goes to it as before.
  - steps past a new entry are dropped where entries are (`commitEntry`'s push, `sameDocumentEntry`'s push, `noteFrameStep`); a step counts only while its entry has the document number it was made with.
- files: new `browser/ui/{frame-step,note-frame-step,drop-frame-steps,frame-step-at,traverse-frames,child-by-key,slot-of-process}.aye`; `browser/ui/{tab-process,new-tab-process,end-tab-process,child-sent,swap-child,service-children,go-to-entry,commit-entry,same-document-entry}.aye`; new `browser/tests/ui/{oopif-history,oopif-history-child}.html`; `tests/browser/window_renderer_limits.rs`.
- Chromium read: `content/browser/renderer_host/navigation_controller_impl.cc` (`RendererDidNavigateNewSubframe`: "the new entry is a clone of the last committed one with the frame's item replaced"; `FindFramesToNavigate`: a traversal navigates the frames whose item differs, same-document ones separately).
- verify (2026-11-19, Linux x86_64): `window_renderer_limits_frame_history`, `oopif-history.html` (a `localhost` frame with a link in a `127.0.0.1` page that counts its loads in its title): a click on the frame's link gives `history 2 of 2`, the child `H?second`, the parent `P loads 1`; `cmd+[` gives `history 1 of 2`, the child `H`, the parent still `P loads 1`; `cmd+]` gives `history 2 of 2` and `H?second` again. - the top renderer is not told the new length either (`history.length` in the parent is one short after a frame step, until its next navigation).

- a step navigates by GET: a frame that got its document by POST is fetched again by GET on Back; the frame's scroll offset is not restored.
- a traversal that leaves the top document and comes back reloads the page, and the frame starts at its `src` again (the steps are kept for the document number, the reloaded document has a new one).
- session restore does not save frame steps.
