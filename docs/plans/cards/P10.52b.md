- goal: WPT area `css/overflow` from 35.1% toward its 60% threshold (P10.52's `left` list), without changing the window's real scrolling.
- cause groups (failing subtests at start, 1237 of 1907): discrete interpolation (`overflow-no-interpolation` 66, `scroll-axis-lock-interpolation` 42: mostly `overflow-clip-margin` and `scroll-axis-lock` unknown to `CSS.supports`); `line-clamp`/`block-ellipsis`/`max-lines`/`continue` layout and parsing (~250); `overflow-clip-margin` parsing, computed values and reftests (~100); scroll markers and `::scroll-button()` (~150, skipped: tentative); `text-overflow` and `<string>` reftests (~45); `scrollbar-gutter` parsing and layout (~55); inline `overflow` shorthand (`overflow-shorthand-001`, 16); reftests whose reference is in `css/reference/` (not in the WPT sparse checkout, so they always failed).
- design: new longhands `overflow-clip-margin` (`<visual-box> || <length>`, computed as text: box first, `padding-box` and a zero length dropped), `scrollbar-gutter` (`auto | stable && both-edges?`), `scroll-axis-lock` (`auto | none`); `overflow-block`/`overflow-inline` are logical aliases of `overflow-y`/`overflow-x`. `text-overflow` takes one or two of `clip | ellipsis | <string>`; the line-end one is used, a string replaces the ellipsis (a line clamp keeps the default ellipsis). `-webkit-line-clamp` takes positive integers only. Inline style (`el.style.x = v`) validates and canonicalizes these through `parseValue`; setting `overflow` writes `overflow-x` and `overflow-y`, and reading `overflow` composes them. Paint: an `overflow: clip` axis clips at the `overflow-clip-margin` box grown by its length; a `visible` axis beside a `clip` one no longer clips. `scrollbar-gutter` reserves nothing: scrollbars are overlay (as on macOS), which the spec exempts. Flex items now get their `position: relative` offset (it was dropped, also on real sites). `wpt.py fetch` adds `css/reference` (shared reference pages).
