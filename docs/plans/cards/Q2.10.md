# Q2.10: out-of-process iframes 2 (input, focus, nested scrolling, intersection)

- goal: with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site`, input over a cross-site iframe goes to the iframe's renderer: the browser hit-tests the child rects it knows from `subframes`, sends mouse and wheel events in the child's coordinates, keeps a drag that started in a child going to it, and sends keys and text to the focused frame. Tab crosses the parent → child → parent boundary both ways, and a parent's `iframe.focus()` moves the keyboard focus into the child. A wheel over the child scrolls the child; what the child cannot use at its end bubbles to the parent (Chromium's scroll bubbling). The child learns its visible rect in the top's viewport, so its `IntersectionObserver` fires when the parent scrolls it into view. Also two tab-mode gaps that Q2.9 hit: a script's `scrollTo` now scrolls the page in a renderer, and a `scrollTo` during the load is no longer overwritten by the history's restored offset. With `DWELLER_PROCESS=tab` the input path is unchanged (no children: every hit is the tab's renderer).
- design:
  - IPC (type codes 44–48, after `subframes` 43):
    - 44 R→B `wheel_unused{seq, dx, dy}`: the part of wheel or `scroll_bubble` `seq` that a child renderer did not use. Sent only by a renderer that got `viewport_intersection` (it knows it is embedded).
    - 45 B→R `scroll_bubble{seq, x, y, dx, dy}`: that rest, to the embedder, at the child's point in the embedder's viewport (CSS px). The embedder runs its scroll chain from that point (inner scrollers, then its page) with no `wheel` event, and acks it as input; if it is a child itself, its own rest bubbles on.
    - 46 R→B `focus_frame{key, how}`: the focus moved to remote frame `key` of this renderer (how 0: a script focused its owner element; 1 Tab; 2 Shift+Tab).
    - 47 B→R `advance_focus{key, reverse}`: continue Tab traversal: key 0 from the frame's start (or end, `reverse`); else onward from remote child `key`, whose renderer sent the existing `take_focus`.
    - 48 B→R `viewport_intersection{x0, y0, x1, y1}`: the child's visible rect (its clip ∩ every embedder's visible clip) in the child's own device px, sent when it changes.
  - browser (`ui/`): `hitFrame` walks a process's children from the last (top-most) one; a child that said hello, lives and showed a frame and whose clip (in the embedder's buffer, moved by the offsets so far) holds the point takes it, recursively; else the embedder (a gone child's sad frame and a child before its first frame go to the parent, which sees the placeholder). `FrameHit{tp, ox, oy}` carries the frame's device offset in the tab; `sendMouse` and `forwardWheel` turn the window point into the frame's CSS px. The top `TabProcess` keeps `mouse_frame` (a move into another frame first sends `mouse{leave}` to the old one, `leaveFrame`), `capture_frame` (set on a button down, `mouseTarget` prefers it, cleared when the last button is up) and `focus_frame` (`focusedFrame`; set by a mouse down, by `focus_frame` and by `take_focus` of a child). Frames are named by their frame id, never by pointer, since a child's `TabProcess` is freed when it ends. `tellFocus` → `tellFrameFocus` gives the existing `focus{on}` only to the focused frame's process and `focus{off}` to the others; a mouse down that moves the focus to another frame tells them before the `mouse` (so the child sees its page focused before its click focuses an element). `forwardKey` and text go to `focusedFrame`. `serviceChildren` bubbles a child's `wheel_unused` (`bubbleScroll`) and hands a child's `take_focus` to its embedder (`focusEmbedder`: `advance_focus{key}`, focus to the embedder). `tellIntersections` (each poll, recursive with the offsets and the visible bound, as `addChildDamage`) sends `viewport_intersection`. `renderer:input` and the poll timeout now wait for every process of the tab to ack and for pending bubbles (`inputWaits`).
  - the browser's checks (a failure kills: `bad message: REASON PID`): a child's `input_ack` as a tab's (`seq` after the last acked, not past the last sent; it was "unexpected in a frame" before); `wheel_unused` only from a child, only for the last wheel or bubble sent to it and once (`seq`), each delta 0 or of the sent delta's sign and at most its size (`unusedWithin`), else `bad wheel_unused` (from a top: `unexpected wheel_unused`); `focus_frame` on the sender's frame id, `how` ≤ 2, `key` one of the sender's own remote frames, else `bad focus_frame`; a child's `take_focus` as before (`reverse` ≤ 1). A renderer can only move the focus into its own children or, by `take_focus`, out to its embedder.
  - renderer (`renderer/`): `wheelInput` dispatches the page's `wheel` listeners as before, then `scrollChain` (inner scroller at the point, an in-process frame, the page) returns the unused part (`unusedPart`: same sign, no more than asked, under 0.5 px is none) and `sayUnused` sends it when embedded; `scrollBubble` runs `scrollChain` alone. `engine.scrollFrameAt` no longer lets a remote placeholder swallow the wheel. Tab order (`engine.focusables`) includes the owner elements of remote frames (only they exist only in `site` mode); `tabKey` that lands on one sends `focus_frame{how 1|2}`; `reportFrameFocus` (after each batch, as `reportPage`) sends `focus_frame{0}` when a script focused one (`iframe.focus()` sets the parent's focused element to the iframe). `advanceFocus`: key 0 clears the focus and tabs to the first (last) focusable, or sends `take_focus` when there is none; key k focuses k's owner quietly and tabs on from it; either way the renderer counts as focused at once, so the browser's following `focus{on}` fires no second `focus` event. `takeIntersection` keeps the rect and gives it to the page (`engine.setVisibleRect`, also after each navigation); it bumps the geometry stamp and runs the layout hook, and the prelude's IntersectionObserver clips an implicit root's intersection by `ak.visibleRect()` (an empty array: nothing visible; null: not a child, as before).
  - tab-mode fixes: `paintFrame` takes the page's script scroll (`engine.takeScriptScroll`) before it lays out, as the in-process window does after scripts; since the load's `sharedHistory` sets the restored offset first, a `scrollTo` during the load wins (in-process `settleScroll`: "where a script of the page scrolled while it loaded, else where the tab is").
  - test hooks: `--events` word `renderer:child-title:END` (waits until the first child frame's title ends with END, `_` a space); `DWELLER_TEST_EVIL=wheel` (a child claims a 1e6 px rest), `=focus-frame` (a renderer names frame 0x7fffffff).
- Chromium read: `components/input/render_widget_host_input_event_router.cc` (`FindViewAtLocation`: the top-most child surface at the point, recursively, the point transformed into it; `mouse_capture_target_` set on a mouse down and kept while a button is down; `SendMouseEnterOrLeaveEvents` when the target changes; `BubbleScrollEvent`: the child's unconsumed scroll goes to the embedder's view at the transformed point), `content/browser/renderer_host/render_widget_host_view_child_frame.cc` (`GestureEventAck` and the input helper: an unconsumed scroll update of a child is bubbled, not re-dispatched as a wheel), `third_party/blink/renderer/core/frame/remote_frame_view.cc` (`UpdateViewportIntersection`, `SetViewportIntersection`: the parent computes the remote frame's visible rect and sends it when it changes; the child's IntersectionObserver uses it for the implicit root), `third_party/blink/renderer/core/page/focus_controller.cc` (`AdvanceFocusAcrossFrames`: a remote frame owner in the Tab order hands the traversal to the frame's process; at a child's end it goes back to the parent from the owner). Dweller takes: rect-based hit testing in the browser with capture and leave, scroll bubbling by the unused delta, focus traversal as two async messages through the browser, and the visible rect pushed to the child.

## Status: done, 2026-11-09

Left:
- hit testing ignores what the parent paints above an iframe (Q2.9's "over" gap), `pointer-events: none` on the iframe, transforms and overflow clips of its ancestors (the browser has no `hit_test` slow path yet); a gone child's sad frame and a child before its first frame pass input to the parent's placeholder.
- wheel latching is per process only: a precise (trackpad) stream that began in the child does not stay latched there in the browser, and every wheel event's rest bubbles (Chromium chains only at the start of a scroll sequence and honors `overscroll-behavior` across frames). Keyboard scrolling (arrows, Space) in a child that is at its end does not bubble. Scroll that an in-process frame cannot use still does not chain to its page (as before, in both modes).
- focus: the parent's focus ring is drawn around the iframe element while the focus is inside it; a click into a child does not move the parent's `document.activeElement` to the iframe (the parent only loses the page focus: `blur` on its focused element); a focused child does not get `blur`/`focus` on its window when the tab or window changes beyond the existing `focus{on}`; in-process same-site frames are still not in the Tab order; Tab with no focusable element in a top page still does nothing (Chromium goes to the toolbar).
- IntersectionObserver in a child: `rootBounds` is the child's own viewport (it should be null for a cross-origin target), `rootMargin` applies to the child's viewport, and a hidden child is not throttled. In-process frames still observe against their own viewport only.
- the click event has no coordinates in the renderer (`clickAt`, as in-process); the test reads them from `pointerdown`/`pointerup`.
- resize: the child's size follows the iframe (Q2.9) and the hit test uses the new rect, but no `resize` event fires at the child's window (none fires in-process either).
- cursor, status URL (link hover) and long-task reports of a child are still checked and dropped; a context menu, `select` popup and drag-selection that leave a child are not routed.
