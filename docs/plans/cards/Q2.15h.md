# Q2.15h: a rule for a site that keeps crashing

- goal: "Not needed for the flip" list under the readiness list of Q2.14, first item of the lane's second list [Q2.5 Left: no "three crashes of one site within 30 s" rule]. The browser tells a site whose renderer keeps crashing from one that crashed once.
- what was there: a crashed renderer leaves the sad tab "This page crashed" with Reload; nothing reloads by itself (Q2.5), so process-model §7's "after three crashes within 30 s Reload is not automatic" had nothing to switch off.
- design: the browser keeps the times of the last three crashes per site (`ui/note-crash.aye`, a fixed table of 16 sites, the oldest slot reused; no allocation). `dropTabProcess` notes one when a renderer process ends as `crashed` (the end of its socket or a failed send; not a kill for a bad message or by Stop), once per process, under the process's site. `keepsCrashing(site)`: the third-last crash is at most 30 s old. The sad tab of such a site says "This page keeps crashing" (`ui/sad-tab.aye`; `--state` prints the heading). Reload is still offered and works: it is the person's act.
- decision: no automatic refusal of Reload and no back-off. Chromium's sad tab does the same: a second failure soon after the first changes the text and offers help (`chrome/browser/ui/sad_tab.cc`, repeatedly crashing), the button still reloads. The rule is the place later automatic restarts (a crashed child frame restarted when it scrolls into view, session restore of a crashed tab) must ask first.
- files: new `browser/ui/{crash-site,note-crash,crash-site-of,keeps-crashing}.aye`; `browser/ui/{drop-tab-process,sad-tab}.aye`; `tests/browser/window_renderer_limits.rs`; `docs/process-model.md` (§7).
- verify (2026-11-19, Linux x86_64; test commands with `env -u HTTPS_PROXY -u https_proxy -u CURL_CA_BUNDLE -u SSL_CERT_FILE`):
  - `window_renderer_limits_crashing`, with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site`, `form.html`: kill, Reload, kill gives `sad-tab This page crashed`; a third kill after another Reload gives `sad-tab This page keeps crashing`; one more Reload shows `title Form` and no sad tab.
  - `cargo test --release --test browser -- window_renderer_limits window_renderer_sad_tab`: 3 passed, 0 failed. The whole suite and the checks: see the line below.
