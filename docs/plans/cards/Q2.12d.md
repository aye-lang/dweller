# Q2.12d: same-site popups in their opener's renderer

- goal: with `DWELLER_SPACES=renderer`, a popup that keeps its opener and is same-site with it is created in its opener's renderer process, as Chromium does, so `window.opener`, `window.name` and synchronous scripting between the two work as in process; the golden agent session `popup` then passes byte-equal (`opener set name w1`) and `window_spaces_in_renderer` runs all 9 sessions. The same code serves popups of the person's tabs under `DWELLER_PROCESS=tab`.
- source: Q2.12's Left, third item; the flip's readiness list ("`opener`, named targets and popup openers across renderers"); `docs/process-model.md` §3 reuse rule 1 ("the same BCG and the same site: its one process (required)").
- why it is three slices: a renderer serves exactly one tab today (one connection, one `TabRenderer` on the stack of `serveOn`, the engine's hooks bound to it), and the browser's `TabProcess` is one connection that owns its pid (`endTabProcess` waits for that pid to exit; a crash is seen per `TabProcess`). Both sides must learn "one process, several tabs" before a popup can be placed in its opener's process.
- slices:
  1. The renderer serves several tabs (done, below). IPC 74 `attach_tab{context}` B→R on the opener's connection carries one socket fd (SCM_RIGHTS, as `resize` carries its buffers); the renderer starts a second `TabRenderer` on that socket that shows popup context `context` (the one its page opened, with its opener and name). One loop polls every tab's connection; the engine's hooks act for the tab being served. Nothing sends 74 yet, so no behaviour changes. Verify: check, unit tests (74 round-trips, goes only to the renderer), taste, layers; `window_renderer`, `window_spaces agent_sessions window_renderer_lifecycle window_login_in_renderer`: 0 failed.
  2. The browser places same-site popups that keep an opener in the opener's process. `Context` says whether the new context keeps its opener (Chromium's `opener_suppressed`); `openedSlot` (hosted Spaces) and the person's tab-mode popups call `attachTabProcess(opener_tp, slot, context)` when the opener is kept and `href`'s site is the opener's committed site in the same partition: a socketpair, 74 with one end, a `TabProcess` whose `host` is the opener's (pid, site lock, partition shared; `endTabProcess` of an attached connection sends `shutdown` and closes only its fd; the pid is waited for when its last connection ends; `pollTabSlot` reaps a shared pid once and marks every tab on it gone). Otherwise a renderer of its own as today. Verify: `window_spaces_in_renderer` 9 of 9 (the `popup` filter removed); `window_spaces agent_sessions window_renderer_lifecycle window_login_in_renderer` and `window_renderer`: 0 failed; a new `window_renderer_popup_opener` (`DWELLER_PROCESS=tab`): a same-site `window.open` popup sees its opener, sets `opener.document.title`, and runs in the opener's pid; a `noopener` and a cross-site popup each get a pid of their own with `opener` null.
  3. Edges of a shared process: an attached tab that navigates cross-site leaves the process (the speculative swap must not end the shared pid; its `opener` becomes null), the opener's tab closes while its popup lives (the process stays), a crash of the shared process fails the requests of every hosted tab on it with "renderer crashed", and `takeContext`'s gesture check for a context request of the attached tab. Verify: one window test per edge, plus the slice 2 tests.
- design as built (slice 1):
  - renderer: `serveOn` keeps a list of `TabRenderer`s on the heap (`newTabRenderer`) and hands it to `serveTabs`: a tab with a whole message waiting is served first (`waitingTab`), else `pollTabs` waits on every tab's connection for at most the nearest timer of any tab (`timerWait`), and on a timeout runs every tab's timers. Before a tab's messages or timers run, `serveAs` points the engine's hooks (fetch, cookies and storage, remote windows, the hang hook) at it. `shutdown`, a bad message or a closed connection ends that tab only (`endTab`, which now closes its connection); the process exits with its last tab, as before for one.
  - `attach_tab{context}` queues the next received fd with the context (`TabRenderer.attaching`); after the batch, `adoptAttached` starts a `TabRenderer` on it whose `context` is set, so its first `navigate` loads into that context (`load` → `setLoadingContext`) and the page has the opener and name its opener gave it. It sends `hello` on the new connection, as a fresh renderer does. The fd is closed instead when the context is unknown, has no opener, already shows a page, is loose, or is another tab's (`attachable`).
  - net's remote loads are keyed process-wide now (`PendingFetch.load`, from `next_load`), since two tabs' wire ids both start at 1; `cancelRemote` and `landFetched` translate.
- IPC: 74 `attach_tab{context}` B→R, with one fd. 75–77 unused.
