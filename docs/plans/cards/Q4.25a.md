- goal: with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site` the zol scroll of `browser/engine/PERF.md` (Q4.25, note 3) ends: each wheel gets the renderer's `input_ack`. On 2026-11-12 (main bd2e1695) 3 of 3 runs hung to the tool's 600 s timeout, the window and the renderer at 0% CPU (`docs/plans/cards/Q4.25.md`).
- source: `docs/plans/cards/Q4.25.md` (d), row Q4.25a of `foundations.md`.
- method: main d8537a82, `browser/build.sh` (safe), Apple M2 Pro, the archives of `target/sites`, 1-minute load 2.2 to 3.3. One `window_perf.py` run at a time, each under a 180 s watchdog that would `sample` the window and the renderer and then kill them (not needed: no run hung). `A=$PWD/target/sites; P="python3 browser/tools/window_perf.py scroll"`:
  - scroll: `DWELLER_PROCESS=site DWELLER_REPLAY=$A/$S $P $URL --step "renderer:loaded wheel:-3 renderer:input" --run-frames 8000 --log L`
  - click: the same with `--frames 10 --step "renderer:loaded click:6,500 release:6,500 renderer:input"`
