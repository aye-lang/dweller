# Q5.11b: IndexedDB 2: indexes, cursors and key ranges

- goal: `createIndex`, `deleteIndex`, `IDBIndex` (`get`, `getKey`,
  `getAll`, `getAllKeys`, `count`, `unique`, `multiEntry`), cursors on
  stores and indexes (`openCursor`, `openKeyCursor`, the four
  directions, `continue`, `continuePrimaryKey`, `advance`, `update`,
  `delete`), `getAll` with options and `getAllRecords`, and renaming
  stores and indexes. `IDBKeyRange` came with Q5.11a.
- verify: WPT `IndexedDB`, 502/1370 (36.6%) before; the Rust tests
  `indexeddb_index`, `indexeddb_cursor` in one process and in
  `window_renderer_indexeddb` (`tab`, `site`).

## Design

On top of Q5.11a (`cards/Q5.11a.md`); Chromium:
`content/browser/indexed_db/indexed_db_cursor.cc`,
`indexed_db_index_writer.cc`,
`third_party/blink/renderer/modules/indexeddb/idb_cursor.cc`,
`idb_index.cc`.

- The page computes a record's index keys (key paths are evaluated on
  the clone) and sends them with `put`: `<index id> <key>` pairs. The
  browser keeps, per index, the entries (index key, primary key) sorted
  by both; an entry borrows its bytes from its record, so a record is
  linked into and unlinked from the indexes whenever it enters or
  leaves its store (put, delete, rollback, load, copy).
- A unique index refuses a `put` whose key another primary key holds
  (`ConstraintError`), before anything is changed.
- One walk serves every read: a view (a store's records, or an index's
  entries) and a step "the next entry after this position in this
  direction inside this range". `get`, `getAll`, `getAllKeys`,
  `getAllRecords` and `count` are that walk with a limit; a cursor
  keeps its position in the page and asks for one step per
  `continue`, so the browser holds no cursor state and a cursor sees
  the writes made since its last step.
- `createIndex` on a store with records: the page reads the records,
  computes their keys and sends them (`set-index-keys`); a unique
  violation aborts the transaction with `ConstraintError`.
- The file format becomes `dweller-idb 2` (indexes per store); a
  version 1 file is ignored.

## Slices

1. Backend: indexes (create, delete, rename, entries, unique check),
   the view and its step, `get`/`get-all`/`count` through it, renaming
   a store, the file format. Verify: unit tests.
2. Front end: `IDBIndex`, `createIndex`/`deleteIndex`/`index`, index
   keys on `put` (`multiEntry`), metadata and its revert on abort.
   Verify: `indexeddb_index`.
3. Front end: `IDBCursor`, `IDBCursorWithValue`, `openCursor`,
   `openKeyCursor` on stores and indexes. Verify: `indexeddb_cursor`.
4. `getAll` options, `getAllRecords` (`IDBRecord`), renames.
5. WPT clusters; the score and the baseline.
