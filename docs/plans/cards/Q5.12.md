# Q5.12: Permissions with a prompt (notifications, geolocation, camera, microphone)

- goal: the four permissions are "prompt" until the person answers a prompt of the browser, the answer is kept per origin in the profile, and the APIs that need them answer as Chromium's do without the permission or without a device. In all three process modes: the store and the prompt are the browser's. Row Q5.12 of `foundations.md` (F1, Chromium `components/permissions/`, `modules/permissions/`).
- facts found first (2026-11-16):
  - None of the APIs exist: no `navigator.permissions`, `Notification`, `navigator.geolocation`, `navigator.mediaDevices` (grep of the preludes). A page that feature-tests them takes its "unsupported" path, which is the right answer for a browser without them, but pages that call them unguarded stop.
  - `isSecureContext` is `true` for every page (`prelude.aye`); `net` has `trustworthyUrl` (not public).
  - The browser's prompts today: the HTTP auth sheet (`ui/auth.aye`), the certificate bar (`ui/cert-bar.aye`, one button) and the stuck bar (`ui/stuck-bar.aye`, two buttons). `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` show nothing (lane feat-page, Q5.3). No per-host decision is saved anywhere (certificate exceptions are the session's).
  - The engine reaches the window through function pointers the window sets (`engine.setHangHook`, `setStorageRemote`, `setRemoteWindows`); a renderer asks the browser synchronously as cookies do (`renderer/cookies-remote.aye`) and gets later answers as `PopupChoice` comes (`renderer/answer-all.aye`).
  - No permission policy (`allow=`, `Permissions-Policy`) exists.
  - WPT: `permissions/`, `notifications/`, `geolocation/`, `mediacapture-streams/` are not in the checkout, and the tests that grant or deny need `test_driver.set_permission`, which `tools/wpt/testdriver-vendor.js` does not have.
- slices (each with its verify):
  1. The store (`net/`, the browser process): `permissionOf(page URL, kind)` (prompt, granted, denied; denied at once for an origin that is not potentially trustworthy or is opaque) and `setPermissionOf`, kept in `<profile>/permissions` (origin, kind, state per line). Verify: Aye unit tests.
  2. The APIs in one process without a prompt host (`engine/permissions.js`, `ak.permissionState`, `ak.permissionAsk`): `navigator.permissions.query` and `PermissionStatus`, `Notification` (`permission`, `requestPermission`, the constructor's `show`/`error`), `navigator.geolocation` (`PERMISSION_DENIED`, or `POSITION_UNAVAILABLE` when granted: there is no location provider), `navigator.mediaDevices` (`NotAllowedError`, or `NotFoundError` when granted: there is no device; `enumerateDevices()` is empty). With no window (`dump`) a request is dismissed: the promise settles as Chromium's does when the prompt is closed, and nothing is stored. Verify: `dump` golden `net/permissions.{html,txt}`.
  3. The prompt in the window (`ui/permission-*.aye`): a bar under the toolbar, "ORIGIN wants to show notifications" with Allow and Block, one request at a time; the answer is stored and the page's promise settles; a navigation dismisses what is pending; `--state` prints `permission ORIGIN KIND`. Verify: a window test that clicks Allow for one permission and Block for another, and a second run with the same profile that is not asked again.
  4. Renderer modes: IPC 145 `PermissionGet{doc, kind}` (sync) answered by 146 `PermissionState{state}`; 147 `PermissionAsk{id, doc, kind}` answered later by 148 `PermissionAnswer{id, state}`. The origin is the browser's record of document `doc`, never the renderer's word. Verify: slice 3's test with `DWELLER_PROCESS=tab` and `site`.
  5. (if room) `test_driver.set_permission` and the WPT areas `permissions` and `notifications`.
- decisions (nobody to ask; the plan's defaults):
  - "Denied until asked": the state is `prompt` (`Notification.permission` is `default`) until an answer; nothing is granted without a click on Allow. A dismissed prompt stores nothing.
  - Granted permissions do not reach a device or the system's notification centre in this row: geolocation answers `POSITION_UNAVAILABLE`, `getUserMedia` `NotFoundError`, and a `Notification` fires `show` without a system notification. Those are Mac-queue items of their own rows (media is Q5.9a).
  - Frames ask for their own origin (no permission policy, no "embedded in" wording): Left.
- references: https://w3c.github.io/permissions/ , https://notifications.spec.whatwg.org/ , https://w3c.github.io/geolocation/ , https://w3c.github.io/mediacapture-main/ ; Chromium `components/permissions/permission_request_manager.cc` (one prompt at a time, the queue, dismissal on navigation), `permission_context_base.cc` (the secure-origin rule, the stored content setting per origin), `third_party/blink/renderer/modules/permissions/permissions.cc`, `modules/notifications/notification.cc`.
