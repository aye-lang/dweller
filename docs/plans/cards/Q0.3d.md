# Q0.3d: bbc's consent requests: full bodies in the archive, and the POST after a nosniff preflight goes out

- goal: the archive of bbc records the responses of `cdn.privacy-mgmt.com` (the consent manager) with their bodies, and the POST `wrapper/v2/pv-data` gets its answer, as in curl and Chrome. These gave bbc 2 console errors (its limit is 1): `ERROR in messagingWithoutDetection: SyntaxError: Unexpected end of JSON input` and `Uncaught (in promise) TypeError: Failed to fetch: network error`.

## Cause

Two bugs in `browser/net/`, one for each error.

1. **The archive dropped the part of a body that the page had already read.** An asynchronous XMLHttpRequest (and a fetch whose body is read as a stream) takes its body in parts while it arrives (`takeStreamPart`, `browser/net/take-stream-part.aye`), and each take clears `job.sink.body`. When the transfer ended, `endRequest` (`browser/net/loader.aye`) recorded `a.res`, whose body is only what came after the last take. A small response that arrived in one piece before libcurl reported the end was recorded as `200` with 0 bytes; the replay then gave the page an empty body and `JSON.parse` threw.
   - Evidence: the old archive has `wrapper/v2/meta-data` with `content-length: 646` and 0 body bytes, and `get_site_data` with 0 bytes. A live `agent` record of `https://www.bbc.com/news` with a debug log in `takeStreamPart` and `finishRequest` showed `wrapper/v2/messages`: part taken 0, then 5030 bytes, then the transfer ended with `body=0`. The record had 200 and no body (1771 bytes, header only). `curl --compressed` gets the 646 bytes of `meta-data`. With `dump` the parts are often taken only after the end, so the bug looked as if it depended on timing.
2. **ORB checked the response of the CORS preflight.** `preflight` (`browser/net/preflight.aye`) sent the `OPTIONS` request without a mode, so it was `no-cors` (the default of `Request`). `request` ran `orbChecked` on it, and ORB blocks a `no-cors` response of `text/html` with `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`. cdn.privacy-mgmt.com (Express) answers every preflight that way. The preflight failed after it was recorded with 200, and the POST or PUT that it allowed failed with "network error" (XHR `pv-data`, fetch `wrapper/metrics/v1/custom-metrics`: the uncaught `Failed to fetch`).
   - Evidence: a debug log in `orbChecked` printed `orb blocked https://cdn.privacy-mgmt.com/wrapper/v2/pv-data?... method=OPTIONS mime=text/html` on a live `dump`. On the old archive the replay printed the same for `wrapper/metrics/v1/custom-metrics`, with the 2 errors. `curl -X OPTIONS` with the page's `Origin` and `Access-Control-Request-*` headers: `HTTP/2 200`, `content-type: text/html; charset=utf-8`, `x-content-type-options: nosniff`, `access-control-allow-origin: https://www.bbc.com`, `access-control-allow-methods: GET, PUT, POST, DELETE`, body `POST`. The fetch spec makes a preflight a request of mode `cors` (https://fetch.spec.whatwg.org/#cors-preflight-fetch), and ORB applies only to `no-cors`.

Ruled out: content encoding (the `meta-data` response is not encoded, `messages` is gzip; both lost their bodies the same way, and curl decodes both); HTTP/2 against 1.1 (curl gets the whole bodies on HTTP/2, and the engine received the 5030 bytes of `messages`: they were lost after the transfer, in the record); a redirect (`res.url` is empty, no `Location`); the cache and `Vary` (`startRequest` loads are never cached; the bodies were gone at `finishRequest`); the body limit (`too_large` is false); the POST body and its `Content-Type` (the POST was never sent: the preflight failed first).

## Fix

- `preflight` sends its `OPTIONS` request with `mode: Mode.Cors` and `no_preflight: true` (a CORS request that needs no preflight of its own). ORB does not check it, and the request sends `Sec-Fetch-Mode: cors`, as Chrome does.
- A script's request keeps the bytes that the page took, only when `DWELLER_RECORD` is set (`Ask.taken`). `archiveStreamed` (`browser/net/archive-streamed.aye`) records the taken bytes followed by the rest. The page still gets each byte once.

## bbc

- Recaptured 2026-11-24 with this binary (`page_load.py capture --site bbc`: 271 requests). Every `cdn.privacy-mgmt.com` response has its body (`meta-data` 646 bytes, `get_site_data` 209, `messages` 5030). `pv-data` POST: 200, 193 bytes, after its preflight 200.
- Console errors of the replay (`agent` `navigate`, as `check` counts them): 1, the known `DataCloneError` of sites.json's note, no archive miss. Before: 2. The limit is 1.
- `check --site bbc` and `check --agent --site bbc` still FAIL. The page load's replay (`dump`) misses 5 to 8 ad and beacon URLs (`securepubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/ads` with other `correlator` values and more `scr_y` refreshes, `elb.the-ozone-project.com/static/load-cookie.html`, `ping.chartbeat.net/ping`, `comcluster.cxense.com/Repo/rep.gif`). Two captures in a row gave such misses. These URLs hold `Math.random()` values and scroll positions. Now that the consent POST works, the ad scripts run after network answers that come at other times live than in the replay. This is the problem of the practice "An agent task's replay can miss beacons a recapture cannot fix", here in the page load. The P1.S4 captures had no misses, because the consent flow stopped at the failed preflight. No `sites.json` expectation was changed.
- The old archive is kept as `target/sites-old-bbc` because `check` does not pass yet.

It is not one of bbc's console errors.
