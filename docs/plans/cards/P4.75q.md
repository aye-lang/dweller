#### P4.75q: github's last full restyles: `:has()` invalidation for inserted subtrees

- goal: github's 3 forced layouts that take `STYLE_FULL` (2 `shared_styling`: the sheet swap of P4.75l refused for `deps_whole`; 1 `sheets`) take the partial path; github's `script_layout` + `style_full` at most half of 4153 ms (P4.75p's fast-build numbers).
- cause: a sheet with a `:has()` that `RestyleDeps` cannot follow (`has_keyless`: any element may be its anchor; `has_sibling`: `:has(+ S)`, `:has(~ S)`) is "whole": `wholeUnseen` refuses a restyle when a noted change may change what such a `:has()` matches, and `keysSee` counts every children change (`CHILDREN_CHANGED`, the insertion or removal of a subtree, a text change) as seen. Inserting github's shadow hosts at `body` and `head` therefore refused both the P4.75l sheet swap and the restyle.
- design (Blink's `NeedsHasInvalidationForInsertedOrRemovedElement`, algorithm only): `noteWhole` also classifies each argument of a whole `:has()` (`addHasFeatures` into `RestyleDeps.whole_has`, a `WholeHas`):
  - an argument with a structural pseudo-class anywhere (`:empty`, `:*-child`, `:*-of-type`, `:nth-*`, `:valid`/`:invalid`, `:focus-within`, `:dir()`, shadow pseudos, `@scope` limits: `isStructural`, `structureIn`) or a compound without a type, id, class or attribute (and no `:is()` whose arguments all have one: `compoundFeatures`, `isFeatures`) cannot be classified: `whole_has.why` (`structural`, `universal`, `nesting`) and every children change stays seen, as before;
  - else each compound gives one feature (`simpleFeature`: id > class > attribute (`[a=v]` with its value, `attrValueKey`) > type, the strongest as in `subjectRank`). The features an element of an inserted or removed subtree must have (`whole_has.features`) are those of the argument's last compound when its inner combinators are all descendant or child (`argumentStart`: then any chain through the subtree ends in it), else of all its compounds; nested `:is()`/`:not()` arguments add all but their last compound. The features of the elements beside it (`whole_has.neighbours`) are those of every compound: a subtree inserted or removed between two siblings changes `+` between them.
  - the DOM natives note what they insert and remove: `appendChild`, `insertBefore`, `replaceChild`, `removeChild`, `remove`, `textContent`, `innerHTML`, `outerHTML`, `document.title` collect the features of each subtree (`noteSubtree`, `noteChildren`, `noteRemoval`; `css.subtreeFeatures`, `elementFeatures`: tag, every attribute name and name=value, id, classes) into `Script.restyle_keys`, and of the element siblings around the place (`noteNeighbours`, `noteInsertion`) into `Script.restyle_neighbour_keys` (`changedChildren`). Any other children change (`changedAt` with "" at a connected node), or one noted while the layout's sheets had no whole `:has()`, sets `Script.restyle_keys_unknown`. Text data changes (`nodeValue`, `data`, `textContent` of a text node) are children changes without elements.
  - `wholeUnseen` checks the children changes once: unseen unless the argument could not be classified, the keys are unknown, or a feature is among the keys (`css.wholeChildrenWhy`). Seen ones log `DWELLER_LAYOUT_WHY deps_whole_children <why>:<argument name>` (`structural`, `universal`, `nesting`, `subtree_unknown`, `feature`, `neighbour`).
  - the `DWELLER_LAYOUT_WHY` flag is read once by any of its notes (`layoutWhyOn`): `noteWholeSelector` did not log before the first `noteFullLayout` (github's first precheck was missing from P4.75l's counts).
