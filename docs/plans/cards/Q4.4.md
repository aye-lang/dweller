# Q4.4: `:has()` invalidation without a whole-sheet restyle

## Status: done, 2026-11-16

- goal: a change that a `:has()` may see restyles the elements that `:has()` was tested on and what their rules reach, not the whole document. Before, a sheet with a `:has()` that `RestyleDeps` could not follow (`has_keyless`, `has_sibling`: `deps.whole`) refused the partial restyle (`deps_whole`, a `STYLE_FULL`) for every change one of those could see, and a `:has()` it could follow made its topmost matching ancestor and all that ancestor's following siblings full roots.
- design (Blink's `AffectedByHas` flags and `InvalidateAncestorsOrSiblingsAffectedByHas`, algorithm only):
  - anchors: `hasMatches` notes every element a `:has()` is tested on, with which one (`noteHasAnchor`: `Styles.has_anchors`, element index to a 64-bit mask of `hasBit(sub_start)`), during `computeStyles` and `restyleSubtrees` (`setHasAnchors`). An element no `:has()` was tested on has no style that depends on one: the other simples of its compound, or the compounds to its right, did not match, and those do not change with what a `:has()` argument sees. Style sharing and the matched cache run the sibling rules and the matching first, so they note too. The map only grows until the next full cascade; a stale entry costs a restyle of one element.
  - what an anchor's rules reach: `walkKeySets` (Q4.3) gives each `:has()` a `KeySet` under `hasKey(sub_start)`, by the place of its compound: nothing more for the subject, `descendants`/`siblings`/`sibling_descendants` with the subject's feature otherwise, also through `:is()` and `:not()`. `KeySets.has_starts` lists them.
  - roots: `restyleRoots` with `anchored` (`css.anchorsFollow`): for each change a `:has()` may see (`anchorsSee`: `css.hasSees` by the argument names of the whole and the followed `:has()`; a children change in a sheet with only whole ones by `wholeChildrenWhy`, the features of P4.75q), `addAnchorRoots` walks up from the changed element: each ancestor, and, when a `:has()` starts with `+` or `~` (`RestyleDeps.has_sibling`), the element's children and the preceding siblings of each. A candidate in `has_anchors` is a shallow root (`addAnchorRoot`), and for each `:has()` in its mask the set's elements are roots (`addSetRoots`). The key-based path (`anchors`, `mayAnchorAnyDepth`, `top`) is used only when anchors do not follow.
  - anchors do not follow (the old refusal and path stay) when a `:has()` argument has a nested selector with a combinator (`looksOutside`: `:has(:is(.a .b))` may look above the anchor), at nesting 16, for a `:has()` in `:nth-child(of S)`, `::slotted()` or `::part()` (its set is `whole`), and with `deep_any`.
  - `restyleSubtrees` styles the roots ancestors first (`rootOrder`, by style depth): a shallow root above another is styled, and its changed values pushed down, before the one below reads them. Before, a root below styled the chain above it again and the root above then saw no change (`has_anchors.html` "first": `letter-spacing` of the rows' parent did not reach the rows; `DWELLER_LAYOUT_CHECK` found it).
  - the root element as a shallow root styles the document again only when its font size or line height changed (`sameRootUnits`: `rem`, `rlh`), or its custom properties or container type as before. Before, any shallow root at `html` was a whole-document restyle; with a keyless `:has()` every ancestor is an anchor, `html` too.

## Left

- A `:has()` whose argument has a nested selector with a combinator keeps the refusal. Blink follows these with `AncestorsOrSiblingsAffectedByHasForSiblingRelationship` and the descendant flags.
- The anchors of a sibling `:has()` are looked for among all preceding siblings of each ancestor and all children of the changed element: a hash lookup each, but every anchor found is styled again (a list of 1000 `li:has(+ .sel)` items restyles the items before the change). Blink bounds this by the argument's sibling distance.
- A change is "seen" by the argument names of all `:has()` together, not of the ones in the anchor's mask.
- `has_anchors` is keyed by node index and never shrinks between full cascades.
