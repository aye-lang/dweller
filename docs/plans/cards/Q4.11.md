Pages under `browser/tests/perf/` (`dump --text --js`): `host_ic.html` 23
collections, p95 2.5 ms; `feed.html` one of 0.8 ms; `article.html`,
`animated.html`, `staged_scripts.html` none. `browser/perf/` holds no
pages (it is the telemetry module).

So two workloads are over 16 ms: `splay` (a live tree of about a million
values: the last collection is 72 ms of marking and 53 ms of sweeping)
and `dom` (19 ms). The sites of PERF.md (youtube-search p95 87 ms, zol
28, youtube 26, bbc 20) are in the Mac queue.

Where the pause goes (`js_gc_mark` and `js_gc_sweep`, new records): on
`splay` marking is 65 to 72 ms of the two largest pauses and sweeping 11
to 53 ms. Marking does not get faster by tracing through pointers
instead of copies (tried: no change; it is cache misses), so both phases
have to be cut into steps.
