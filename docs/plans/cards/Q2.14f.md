# Q2.14f: the Spaces tests under the flag

- goal: Q2.14a's proposed readiness row 6 (cause rows 9 and 12, 6 tests): the Spaces tests count Space processes without the window's own renderer, and a Space session's `--state` golden holds with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site`.
- source: `docs/plans/cards/Q2.14a.md` rows 9 and 12.
- design:
  - the count: `spaces_of(pid)` counted every child of the window whose command line has ` --renderer 3 `, and with the flag the person's tab has a renderer too. A renderer started for a Space's tab now has `--space-tab` in its arguments (`ui/start-tab-process.aye`: the tab id is in the Space range, `FIRST_SPACE_TAB` up to `FIRST_CHILD_FRAME`; the renderer ignores the word), and `spaces_of` matches ` --renderer 3 .* --space-tab`. It also tells a person reading `ps` which renderers are an agent's.
  - the golden: the `renderer ...` lines are no longer part of `--state` without `--processes` (Q2.14b), so `agents/spaces/*.out` needs no change.
- IPC: none.
- files: `browser/ui/{start-tab-process,host-tab,start-child}.aye`, `tests/browser/window_spaces.rs` (`spaces_of`).
- verify (2026-11-16, the same runs as Q2.14b; the whole suite with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site`, 217 / 263):
  - of the 6 tests, 3 pass: `window_spaces_crash_in_renderer`, `window_spaces_isolated_in_renderer`, `window_spaces_parallel_in_renderer`. Of Q2.14a's row 3, `window_spaces_control_in_renderer`, `window_spaces_crash` and `window_spaces_parallel` pass too, and so do `window_spaces_responsive` and `window_spaces_responsive_in_renderer` of row 11.
  - `window_spaces_in_renderer`: 6 of 9 sessions pass (0 of 8 before). `window_spaces`: 1 of 9. No session differs by a `renderer` line any more; what differs is under Left.
  - the whole suite in the default mode: 263 / 263.
