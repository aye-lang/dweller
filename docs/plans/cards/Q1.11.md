# Q1.11: the 1 h soak (scoreboard S4)

- goal: one window browses the recorded archives for an hour; its memory stays under a budget, and the leak check finds nothing at exit.
- machine: this Mac (arm64, macOS 26), branch `n23-Q1.11` from `main` b738b274, `aye build browser --mode safe --leak-check -o target/dweller-soak` (the shipped safe mode with the allocator's leak report), `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`, the default process mode (site), a fresh `DWELLER_PROFILE` (`~/.dweller` mtimes unchanged before and after).

## Budget (decided)

- After a warm-up of one full cycle over the sites, the total RSS of the window and every process under it, at the end of each later cycle, grows at most 10% and at most 100 MB from the end of cycle 1, over the hour.
- `--leak-check` reports no leak; no crash, hang or sad tab.
- Reported too: the peak RSS and the most processes under the window.

## Tool: `browser/tools/soak.py`

- `soak.py [--minutes 60] [--binary target/dweller-soak] [--dwell 40] [--site NAME] [--out DIR]`, standard library only.
- The window is `--agent-socket S --exit-when-idle --events SCRIPT`: `--frames` is capped at 16384 iterations (about 3 min), and `--exit-when-idle` lets `--events` run without it. The tool connects with the token and sends nothing, so no Space starts; when it hangs up the window quits normally and prints its leak report.
- `DWELLER_REPLAY` points at a temp directory of links to the responses of all 20 judged sites of `sites.json` (an archive names each response by its URL's hash, so they do not collide).
- A cycle, per site: Cmd+T, the URL typed (`text:` words of at most 31 bytes; a URL with `_`, which `text:` types as a space, goes through a `data:` page that `location.replace`s itself), Return, `renderer:loaded`; three wheel notches of 10 down and three up, each waiting for `renderer:input`; Tab and Return (the page's first link, mostly the site's own skip link or logo); at the first site back and forward once (Cmd+[, Cmd+]). The three newest tabs close when four are open, and all but the first tab at the cycle's end, so 1 to 4 tabs are open and every cycle ends in the same state. A marker tab (a `data:` page) then waits `--dwell` seconds and logs `soak-cycle-N` (`DWELLER_CONSOLE=1`); the dwell keeps the script under `ARG_MAX` (553 KB for 92 cycles).
- RSS (`ps -o rss=`) of the window and of every process under it every 10 s and at each marker, to `soak.csv`; stderr to `soak.log`, each line after the seconds since the start; leak lines (`FILE:LINE:COL: leak: N bytes`) split into those before the hang-up (renderers that ended) and after it (the exit). Crashes: new reports of the binary in `~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports`, panics, `bad message`, `stuck:`, a sad tab or gone renderer in `--state --processes`, the window ending early. A hang: no marker for dwell + 300 s.

## Cause of the failure

Renderer processes print their own leak report when they exit after `shutdown` (their stderr is the window's). Runs of one cycle with `--site` (`--minutes 0 --dwell 3`):

- `--site bbc`: 3 leaks of 4 to 6 bytes at `std/text/text.aye:487:34` (`toOwned`), each time bbc's renderer ends; rustdoc, cnn-lite, react, mistral, youtube, youtube-search and zol alone: none.
- `--site react --site mistral` (also with youtube-search first): 12 leaks at `std/list/list.aye:102:39` (a `List` growing: 2560 + 512 bytes four times, 5120 + 512 twice) when the two tabs close; react alone and mistral alone: none.
- the report names only the std line that allocates, not the browser code that called it, so the owners are not found yet. Not a small fix.
