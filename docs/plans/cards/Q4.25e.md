# Q4.25e: the window scrolls the renderer's last frame while the renderer is busy

- goal: with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site` a wheel moves the page at once: the window shifts the renderer's last frame by the wheel and presents it, and the renderer's own frame at the new scroll replaces it when it comes. Before, the scrolled pixels waited for the renderer's task (bbc: up to 5 s).
- source: `docs/plans/cards/Q4.25.md` (c) and (d), row Q4.25e of `foundations.md`.
- slices:
  1. the window shifts the last frame (c2 of Q4.25's note), for pages where that is safe; the strip the frame does not hold shows the page's canvas color. Done, 2026-11-17.
  2. a scroll moves the pixels that are drawn, in the renderer's buffer and in the window's canvas, and draws only the band that came into view. Done, 2026-11-19.
  2b. pixels outside the viewport (c1): the renderer paints a taller buffer, so the strip has content while the renderer is busy.
  3. where it is not safe today (c3): the regions with wheel listeners, fixed and sticky boxes as their own buffer, inner scrollers.
- design, slice 1:
  - IPC: one new message, kind 120 (this lane's range): R→B `scroll_info{max_y, pan, background}`. A tab's top renderer sends it from `paintFrame` (`sayScrollInfo`) when it changes: how far the page scrolls (CSS px), whether the window may shift (`pan`), the canvas color (RGBA). `pan` is 1 while the layout has no fixed or sticky box (`Tree.scrolled` empty) and no wheel of this page was cancelled by a listener or taken by an inner scroller or a frame (`TabRenderer.pan_off`, set in `wheelInput` and `scrollChain`, cleared by a new page). A child renderer never sends it; the browser kills one that does (`unexpected in a frame`). The browser checks it (`takeScrollInfo`: `max_y` finite and not negative, `pan` 0 or 1, else `bad scroll_info`).
  - window: `TabProcess.frame_y` (the scroll of the frame shown, from `frame`), `want_y` (where the window has the page), `max_y`, `pan_ok`, `pan_bg`. `forwardWheel` sends the wheel as before and, when it goes to the tab's top renderer, `panAhead` moves `want_y` by the wheel's `dy`, clamped to 0..`max_y`. `copyTabFrame` copies the shown buffer `tabPan` device px up or down (`want_y − frame_y`, both snapped as the renderer snaps), fills the rows the buffer does not cover with `pan_bg` (`fillPanGap`) and moves the child frames with it. A `frame` sets `frame_y` (`noteFrameScroll`); with no input open it also sets `want_y`, so a script's scroll is followed. The last `input_ack` sets `want_y` to the frame's scroll (`noteInputAcked`): the renderer has the last word, and a wheel it did not use snaps back once (it also turns `pan` off for that page).
  - records: `ui_pan` when a shifted frame is copied, `input_to_pan` from a wheel that moved `want_y` to the window's next present. `window_perf.py scroll` prints `input_to_pan_n_p50_p95`; `report --replay --process site` judges the wheel by it when every wheel was shifted, else by `input_to_frame`.
- Chromium read: `cc/input/input_handler.h` (`ScrollBegin`, `ScrollUpdate` on the compositor thread; `HasBlockingWheelEventHandlerAt` decides when the main thread sees the wheel first), `cc/trees/layer_tree_host_impl.cc` (the scroll moves the pending tree's snapshot; missing tiles are drawn as the background color, `CalculateRenderPasses`). Dweller takes: the window owns the root scroll during a wheel, shows the old pixels moved, and paints the canvas color where it has none.

- design, slice 2:
  - renderer: `frameDamage` no longer damages the whole buffer when the page scrolled vertically by less than its height: `TabRenderer.shift` is the move in device px, the damage is the band that came into view (`addScrollBand`), the layers that cross the edge (`paint.edgeLayers`) and the list's own changes (`paint.diff`), as the window's `drawPage` does in its own process. `drawDamage` copies the last frame moved by `shift` (`copyShifted`) before it draws the damage. A frame with a caret or find marks, a sideways scroll and a new background are still whole.
  - window: `TabProcess.canvas_px` is the scroll (device px) the page canvas holds; `tabShownPx` is the one it should show (the frame's scroll plus the shift ahead of slice 1). `rollTab` (in `drawPage`) rolls the canvas ring by the difference and damages the band; `addTabDamage` moves the frame's damage rects by the shift ahead. So neither a wheel shifted ahead (`panAhead`) nor the renderer's scrolled frame draws the whole tab any more: `noteFrameScroll`, `noteInputAcked` and `takeScrollInfo` no longer set `whole`; `pollTabSlot` repaints when `canvas_px` is behind.
  - a child frame's new place (`subframes`) can arrive one loop turn before its parent's scrolled frame; the frame that follows a child's move is drawn whole (`TabProcess.placed`), else the band alone would leave the child drawn at a place between the two (seen as `window_renderer_oopif_pixels` failing 2 runs in 8).

- design, slice 2b (2026-11-22):
  - a tab's renderer paints `over` rows above and below its viewport (half the viewport's height, `ui/pan-over.aye`; a frame's renderer none): the browser gives buffers of `height + 2 * over` rows and says `over` in `resize` (a new field; the renderer refuses `over > height`). The renderer draws into the buffer through `bufferView` (its view moved down by `over`), its marks moved the same way (`bufferRect`), and its frames' damage rects are buffer rows (`ipc.checkFrame` takes `Buffers.rows()`).
  - the window copies the buffer from row `over` (`copyShown`, `blendShown`, `drawHostedFrame`), maps a frame's damage up by `over` (`addTabDamage`), and fills the canvas color only past the margin (`fillPanGap`): a wheel shifted ahead by up to `over` rows shows the page, not a blank strip. `--state` prints damage in viewport rows.
  - what a frame changes outside the viewport (in the margin) is not drawn at once: the renderer keeps those rects (`TabRenderer.stale`, at most 8, `splitDamage`), moves them with a scroll (`shiftStale`) and draws the part a scroll brings into the viewport at once; the rest is drawn in a frame of its own when the page is settled (`prepaint`, `runTimers`), as Chromium rasters prepaint tiles after the visible ones. The band a scroll brings into the buffer is drawn at once. `DWELLER_DAMAGE_CHECK` skips the stale rects.

- design, slice 3a (2026-11-22): the regions where a wheel must reach the page first. The renderer says them in `scroll_info` (a new field `regions`, CSS px of the viewport as u32 quads, at most 64; the browser refuses a ragged or longer list): each element that scrolls (`engine.wheelRegions`: a scroller box with a scroll range), each same-process frame whose document scrolls, and each element with a wheel listener that is not passive (the prelude keeps them, `watchWheel`; `__wheelRegions` gives their rects; one on the window, the document, its root or its body makes the whole page wait, `pan` 0). The window shifts the page ahead only for a wheel outside them (`inPanRegions`). The renderer turns the shifting off for the page (`pan_off`) only when a wheel it handled itself was outside the regions it said (`saidRegionAt`), so a wheel over an inner scroller or a cancelling listener no longer turns it off.
- verify, slice 3a: new `window_renderer_pan_regions` (`ui/pan_regions.html`, a 20 s script: a wheel outside the regions is shifted ahead, one over the inner scroller or over the cancelling element is not, and after the scroller took a wheel the page still shifts ahead); `window_renderer`, `input::`, `paint::` 119 of 119; `aye test browser --module ipc` 51.

- design, slice 3b (2026-11-22): fixed and sticky boxes as layers of their own. The display list marks the promoted layer of each box in `Tree.scrolled` (`Promoted.pinned`). A tab's renderer (`findPins`) takes them apart when every one is a layer (`!unpinned`), each has a scroll range in which it keeps its place in the viewport (`layout.stuckRange`: a fixed box always, a sticky one with `top` or `bottom` while it sticks; one with both is not taken), at most 8 fit in `over` rows stacked, nothing painted after one of them covers it (`paint.pinLayers`), and no find, field or bubble mark is drawn. Then it paints its buffer without them (`Reuse.skip_pinned`) and each one alone, on a clear background, into `over` more rows below the buffer (`drawPin`; the browser gives `height + 3 * over` rows, `Buffers.mapped`). `frame` gets a field `pins`: per layer x, y (buffer row), width, height, its row below the buffer, and its range (device px of scroll; the browser refuses a pin outside the buffer or its rows, `ipc.checkPins`). The window blends each pin over the shifted frame where it is at the wheel's scroll (`pinPlace`: moved with the page outside its range, kept inside), before the child frames, and damages where it drew pins last (moved by a roll) and where it draws them now (`addPinDamage`). `pan` is 1 again for such a page.

Left:
- slice 3b's limits: a page whose fixed or sticky box is transformed, clipped, inside a frame, covered by later content, sticky on both edges, or taller than `over` together with the others is still not shifted ahead; a box painted after a pin that a shift moves under it is drawn below it until the renderer's frame; a pin's translucent pixels are blended by the window (`overPixel`), which can differ by 1 from the renderer's own blend. The regions see only listeners added with `addEventListener`, not an `onwheel` handler, and a scroller inside another scroller is placed without the outer one's scroll offset. A wheel that shifts ahead by more than `over` rows still shows the canvas color past them.
- a child frame's damage is not moved by the shift ahead (`addChildDamage`), and the frame after a child's move is whole.
- a mouse position sent while the frame is shifted is in the window's coordinates, and the renderer reads it at its own, older scroll; the child frames' hit-test rects are not shifted either.
- only the vertical scroll is shifted; a page that also scrolls sideways shifts vertically only.
- a wheel the page handles outside the regions it said (a listener added since its last `scroll_info`) snaps back once, and then `pan` is off for the page.
- keys that scroll (space, arrows, Page Down) and the scroll bubbled up from a child are not shifted.
- found while verifying slice 3b: `DWELLER_DAMAGE_CHECK` on `ui/pan_busy.html` with `--font fixed` and three wheels of 120 px reports 48 pixels: the bottom of a descender (`y + 0.2 em`, 2.4 px at 12 px) is rounded on the other side of .5 when the same text is drawn 360 rows higher in the buffer, so a scrolled frame and a whole drawing differ by one row of a glyph. It is there since slice 2 (the pixels a scroll moves are kept) and is not about pins; the fix is to round a glyph's edges from a rounded baseline in `drawFixed` and `drawSystem`, which moves every golden by up to a pixel.
