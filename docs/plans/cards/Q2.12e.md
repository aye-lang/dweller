# Q2.12e: the Spaces flip (hosted Spaces are the only Spaces)

- goal: every Space is hosted in the window as a group of renderer-backed tabs (Q2.12, Q2.12b, Q2.12c, Q2.12d); the `DWELLER_SPACES` switch goes, and so does the Space process: `dweller space`, the relay, the control connection, the sharing of cookies and `localStorage` with a Space, the frame file, and the in-Space half of the window program (`Inside`).
- source: Q2.12's row ("then the flip and the removal of the process path"); Q2.12's and Q2.12c's Left ("Then flip"); the wave of 2026-11-16, lane proc-spaces.
- slices:
