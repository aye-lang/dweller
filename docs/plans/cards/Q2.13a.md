# Q2.13a: the dead list buffers in the style sheet's arena

## Status: done, 2026-11-26

- goal: Q2.13's "Mac, real sites" found that the lists of a `css.Sheet` grow inside its arena, which never frees the buffer a list grows out of. Build the growing lists outside the arena and put only final slices in it, so that the arena holds live bytes; youtube-search's renderer (1.7 GB footprint) should get smaller, CSS parse time not slower than 3 %.
- first stopped (below, "On main 1c259ff9"): the CSS live bytes halved, but youtube-search's footprint rose on macOS, as malloc kept the freed list buffers. Re-measured on top of #450 (Q2.13b, one parse per source, 3 parses of `kevlar_base` a load instead of 22): the footprint now falls on every site measured ("On top of #450"). The 1.6 GB of the goal is not reached on macOS: main is at 2.3 GB since #450, and malloc's large cache holds 400 to 500 MB of it (Q2.13c).

## Before (confirmed, main 1c259ff9)

- youtube-search's renderer: footprint 1774, 1782, 1648, 1649, 1625 MB (5 runs).
- CSS holds 285 MB of live malloc blocks, 272 MB of them arena chunks. The arena chunks by site: the token list (`css::tokenize` < `tokens`) 96 MB, `decls` (`Declarer.add` and `addRaw`) 54, `simples`, `compounds` and `complexes` 25, the selector parser's own lists (`parseComplex`, `parseSelectorList`) 35, `components` 15, the rules 3, gradient checks 2.
- cause: `List` doubles with `resize`, which allocates the new buffer and frees the old, and an arena's free does nothing. So each list leaves every buffer it grew out of: about as many dead bytes as its last buffer. Lists that live only during a parse (a selector's simples, a declaration's components) stay too.
- youtube-search parses its 3.85 MB `kevlar_base` sheet 22 times during the load (the author sheet is rebuilt on each change, `engine/swap-sheets.aye`, and up to 8 old sheets are kept): 22 x 46 to 50 ms of `css_parse`.

## Change

- the tokens of a source grow in a list from `sheet.alloc` and are copied into the arena once, at their count (`settled.aye`).
- the lists of `Sheet` (`rules`, `complexes`, `compounds`, `simples`, `decls`, `imports`, `import_layers`, `font_faces`, `keyframes`, `layers`, `conditions`, `registered`, `scoped`, and #450's `parts`) use `sheet.alloc` and are freed in `Sheet.deinit` (`free-sheet-lists.aye`).
- the selector parser's lists (`parseSelectorList`, `parseComplex`, `parseForgiving`, `:has()`) use `sheet.alloc` and are freed on return.
- `components` counts first (`component-count.aye`) and allocates its slice once, at its length.
- `isValidImage` reads a gradient in an arena of its own, freed on return (the result was thrown away in the sheet's arena).
- test: `sheet-bytes_test.aye` parses a generated sheet of 8000 rules (1.4 MB, 27.2 MB of source and tokens) and checks that the arena, without the sheet's lists, holds at most 1.2 x its source and tokens. Before: 148.4 MB (5.5 x), fails. After: 30.2 MB (1.11 x), passes.
