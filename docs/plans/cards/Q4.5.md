# Q4.5: sheet insertion and change invalidate by rule

## Status: done, 2026-11-16

- goal: a style sheet that changes after the first layout restyles the elements its changed rules match, whichever way it changed. P4.75l did this for style and link elements (`swapSheets`: the old and the new author sheet are compared, the subjects of the rules that differ are marked `RULES_CHANGED`). Left of it:
  1. every CSSOM change (`insertRule`, `deleteRule`, a rule's `style`, `replace`/`replaceSync`, `sheet.disabled`, `adoptedStyleSheets` on a connected root) was a change "anywhere" (`sheetsChanged` and `akSetAdopted` called `noteLayoutChangeAnywhere`): `restyle_all`, a full cascade and a full layout each time;
  2. the old sheets a swap keeps alive were kept by the page until it closed, 8 at most: after 8 swaps every sheet change was `sheet:kept`, a full cascade, for the rest of the page's life;
  3. a changed rule marked every element that matches the strongest simple selector of its subject: one rule `.r0 .cell` marked all 200 `.cell`;
  4. a rule without such a simple (`.row > *`, `:root`) was `sheet:universal`, a full cascade.
- design (Blink: `StyleEngine::ScheduleInvalidationsForRuleSets`, `InvalidateForRuleSetChanges`, `ApplyRuleSetChanges`; algorithm only):
  1. `noteSheetsChange`: a CSSOM change moves the layout's version and counts as noted (`layout_noted`), with `style_version` as before; it sets no `restyle_all`. The next forced read finds the changed sheet key (`takeSheetChanges`, which mixes in the CSSOM generation and the adopted sheets) and swaps; a swap that falls back gives the `sheets` refusal and the full path, as for a style element.
  2. the kept sheets belong to the styling they were swapped out of (`Styling.kept`, `keepSheet`) and are freed with it (`dropKeptSheets` in `Page.dropStyling` and `dropComputed`, before the styling's own author sheet, which has the same users). The cap of 8 is per styling: the full path that follows `sheet:kept` makes a new styling, and the next 8 changes swap again.
  3. `markSubjects` marks an element when the whole selector of a changed rule matches it (`anyTarget`: the subject's simple first, then `matchFrom`), in the old sheet for a removed rule and the new one for an added one. Not for a selector that needs the cascade's context or history (`needsCascade`: `:has()`, whose anchors must be noted on every candidate (Q4.4), `@scope`, `:host()`, `::slotted()`, `::part()`): those mark by the subject's simple as before.
  4. a selector without a subject simple is matched whole against every element. A change whose rules match more than half of the document's elements (64 or more) is still `sheet:universal`: a full cascade costs less than that many roots.
- files: new `browser/engine/note-sheets-change.aye`, `changed-sheets.aye`, `keep-sheet.aye`, `kept-sheets.aye`; `browser/css/rule-target.aye`, `needs-cascade.aye`, `any-target.aye`; rewritten `browser/engine/drop-kept-sheets.aye`, `browser/css/rule-subjects.aye`, `mark-subjects.aye`; wiring in `engine/cssom.aye` (1 line), `engine/components.aye` (`akSetAdopted`, 1 line), `engine/swap-sheets.aye`, `engine/engine.aye` (`Styling.kept`; `Page.kept_sheets` removed: 5 lines), `engine/window_api.aye` (`dropComputed`, 1 line), `css/sheet-change-targets.aye`.
