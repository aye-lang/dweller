# Q4.6c: forced reads inside a fixed box lay out only that box's content

- goal: on zol, a geometry read after the scroll-spy's class toggle lays out only what the toggle changed, not the whole document, and no longer falls back to a full layout at `relayout:dropped`. Q4.21 ranked it first (geometry reads force a relayout, 4093 ms per 10 s) and fifth (153 full layouts after `relayout:dropped`, 1185 ms per 10 s).
- method: the Q4.21 method. `browser/build.sh` (safe) builds with aye 5180d0c; before = main c0231194 (the `q421` worktree's `target/dweller`), after = this branch. Zol W7 scroll replay (`--frames 50`, 3 `frame` then 40 `wheel:-3 frame`, `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`, `DWELLER_TIMING=1 DWELLER_LAYOUT_WHY=1`). For the reasons, a throwaway build that logged, for each `relayoutDirty`, the dirty element, the box it climbed to and the boxes built again (not committed).

## Why each forced read laid out (zol scroll, one run, before)

| reason | count | `layout_dirty` / `layout_full` ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `li` toggled `active` in the side nav of `div.guide-widget` (`position: fixed; width: 98px`): the `li` is a list item, its `ul` is under a fixed box, so `canRebuild` refuses both and `relayoutDirty` climbs to `body` and builds all 7575 boxes again | 297 | 2797 |
| the same toggle once the tree is mostly dropped boxes (`relayout:dropped`, `boxes > 2 × built + 1024` after two whole-`body` rebuilds): full layout | 153 | ~1300 (full) |
| images arriving in `ul.pics-list` and `section.scroll-position-mol` (93–813 boxes each) | 96 | 544 |
| reads after a change with nothing to lay out (restyle only) | 930 | 0.3 |

The `li` toggles (`li.active` adds a `::before` and a value layout reads) are 450 of the 1540 reads that laid out, and ~4100 of the run's 4718 ms of `layout_dirty` and `layout_full`.

## Fix

- `relayoutBoundary` (layout/relayout-boundary.aye, Blink `core/layout/layout_object.cc` `ObjectIsRelayoutBoundary`): an absolutely or fixed positioned box whose width does not depend on its content (`width` a length, or both `left` and `right`; `min-width`/`max-width` lengths, `auto` or `none`).
- `blockFlowUp` stops at such a box above the box laid out again: the boxes under it can be built and laid out again in the containing block of the last layout. `relayoutUp` stops (full layout) when it would lay out the boundary itself, so the boundary is never laid out alone: while the content inside comes out with the same size, its position and size are those of the last layout.
- For zol, the toggled `li`'s `ul` (30 boxes) is built and laid out again, not `body`.
