#### P4.75l: style sheet changes after layout restyle only what the changed rules can match

- goal: github's 3 forced layouts that take `STYLE_FULL` because style sheets changed after the page's layout (P4.75k's reason (a): 2 `shared_styling`, 1 `sheets`) take a partial restyle; github's `script_layout` + `style_full` at most half of P4.75g's 6843 ms.
- cause: a changed sheet source key (`sheetSourceKey`) dropped the layout's styling: the shared layout through `layoutStyling` (`shared_styling`), the scripts' own through `computedStyling` (a new `Styling`, then the `sheets` refusal). Nothing compared the old rules with the new ones.
- design: before a forced layout (`geometry`, after its early return) and in `computedStyling`, `takeSheetChanges` checks the key of the sheets the layout's styling has (the page's for a shared layout, `Script.computed` for the scripts' own). When it changed, `swapSheets` parses the author sheet again and `css.sheetChangeTargets` compares old and new: `@font-face`, `@layer`, `@container` conditions and `@property` must be the same (else `sheet:font_face`, `sheet:layer`, `sheet:container`, `sheet:property`, a full restyle as before); a shadow tree's sheet that is new, gone or different marks its host (only its scope; a new shadow sheet with `@font-face`/`@property` falls back: `sheet:scoped_font_face`); for the document's sheet the rules that differ are those between the equal first and equal last rules (`changedRules`, structural `sameRule`); each of their selectors gives the strongest simple of its rightmost compound (id > class > attribute > type, `subjectSimple`); a selector without one (universal, pseudo-classes only, `::slotted`/`::part`) falls back (`sheet:universal`). Every element of the document tree that matches one of those simples (`markSubjects`) is noted with the new change kind `css.RULES_CHANGED` (its own style and what inherits from it: `onlyOwnStyle`, a local change for `localChanges`), and the restyle then styles them as shallow roots. The old author sheet stays alive in `Page.kept_sheets` (the old values and boxes point into it; at most 8 swaps a page, then `sheet:kept`), and the layout's `RestyleDeps` are rebuilt from the new sheet. A swap is not tried when the restyle would be refused anyway for `deps_whole` (`wholeUnseen`), so it costs nothing on those.
