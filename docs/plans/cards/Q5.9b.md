# Q5.9b: Media Source Extensions

- goal: a page can build the media of a `<video>` or `<audio>` itself, as YouTube does: `MediaSource`, `SourceBuffer` and `SourceBufferList` as in https://w3c.github.io/media-source/ , over the demuxers and the decoder process of Q5.9a. Row Q5.9b of `foundations.md` (F1, Chromium `third_party/blink/renderer/modules/mediasource/`, `media/filters/chunk_demuxer.cc`, `source_buffer_state.cc`, `frame_processor.cc`).
- facts found first (2026-11-19):
  - Nothing of the API exists (grep of the preludes): no `MediaSource`, `SourceBuffer`, `srcObject`, `ManagedMediaSource`. `URL.createObjectURL` takes a `Blob` only (`prelude.aye`).
  - Q5.9a's element plays from one resource: byte ranges in the page (`m.bytes`), tracks and samples from `ak.mediaTracks` (the demuxers of `media/` read a whole file: MP4 `moov` sample tables, Matroska `Cluster`s), and two sinks that ask `parts.slice(m, offset, size)` for a sample's bytes. The ready state follows the byte ranges.
  - `media/` cannot read a stream in pieces: no `moof`/`trun`/`tfdt`/`mvex` (fragmented MP4), and `webmProbe` wants `Tracks` and every `Cluster` inside one buffer.
  - The only codecs that decode here are the test backends (PCM, uncompressed RGBA in Matroska); VP9, Opus, H.264 and AAC wait for the Mac's decoders (Q5.9a slice 6). So what the cloud can verify is the API, the byte stream parsers, the buffering model and playback of the uncompressed clip appended in pieces.
  - WPT `media-source/` is not in the checkout; its tests append `test.webm` (VP8/Vorbis or VP9/Opus) and `test.mp4` (H.264/AAC), which do not decode here.
- slices (each with its verify):
  1. The byte stream parsers in `media/` (pure): the WebM byte stream format (an initialization segment: EBML header, `Segment`, `Info`, `Tracks`; media segments: `Cluster` with its `Timecode` and blocks, of known or unknown size) and the ISO BMFF one (`ftyp`+`moov` with `mvex`/`trex`; `moof` with `mfhd`, `traf`: `tfhd`, `tfdt`, `trun`, and `mdat`), both incremental: `segments(bytes)` says how much of the appended bytes is whole segments and gives their tracks and coded frames (time, duration, key, byte range). Verify: Aye unit tests with streams built by hand, cut at every byte.
  2. The API (`engine/media-source.js`): `MediaSource` (`readyState`, `duration`, `addSourceBuffer`, `removeSourceBuffer`, `endOfStream`, `sourceBuffers`, `activeSourceBuffers`, `isTypeSupported`, `setLiveSeekableRange`, the events `sourceopen`, `sourceended`, `sourceclose`), `SourceBuffer` (`appendBuffer`, `updating`, `buffered`, `timestampOffset`, `appendWindowStart`/`End`, `mode`, `abort`, `remove`, `changeType`, the events `updatestart`, `update`, `updateend`, `error`, `abort`), `SourceBufferList`; `URL.createObjectURL(mediaSource)` and `srcObject`; attaching to an element opens the source. The exceptions and the event order of the standard's algorithms. Verify: a `dump` golden of the API without any media data.
  3. The segment parser loop and the coded frame processing: appended bytes go through slice 1's parser; frames go into track buffers (append window, `timestampOffset`, `segments` and `sequence` mode, overlaps replace), `buffered` is their ranges, `remove()` and the duration change drop frames, `endOfStream()` sets the duration to the highest end. `HAVE_METADATA` after the first initialization segment; the element's ready state follows the ranges under the position. Verify: `dump` golden with `clip.mkv` cut into its initialization segment and clusters: `buffered` after each append, a gap, `remove`, `endOfStream`.
  4. Playback: the sinks of Q5.9a take a frame's bytes from the track buffers; the clock stops at the end of what is buffered (`waiting`) and goes on after the next append; a seek into what is not buffered waits for it; `ended` after `endOfStream`. Verify: the clip appended in pieces plays with the right frame at each time, in `dump` and in the window with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site`.
  5. WPT area `media-source` with its baseline (threshold 0, reported), and `MediaSource.isTypeSupported` by what the decoders accept.
- decisions (nobody to ask; the plan's defaults):
  - The type strings follow Q5.9a's decision: without a platform decoder `isTypeSupported('video/webm; codecs="vp9"')` is `false` and `addSourceBuffer` throws `NotSupportedError`, as a browser without the codec does. The uncompressed test clip is `video/x-matroska; codecs="V_UNCOMPRESSED, A_PCM/INT/LIT"`: a type only the tests use.
  - The buffers live in the page (as Q5.9a's bytes do), with Chromium's limits as the eviction target: 150 MB of video and 12 MB of audio a `SourceBuffer` (`QuotaExceededError` when the coded frame eviction cannot make room).
  - No `MediaSource` in workers, no `ManagedMediaSource`, no `SourceBuffer.appendEncodedChunks` in this row.

- slice 1, as built (2026-11-19): the byte stream parsers.
  - `media.webmStream(init, bytes, base, out)` and `media.mp4Stream(init, bytes, out)` read the bytes appended so far from their start and fill a `Stream`: `used` (how many bytes are whole and done with), the coded frames of the media segments among them (`Frame`: the track's number, its byte range in the bytes, time, duration, key), and where an initialization segment is when one ends at `used` (the caller keeps it and passes it back as `init`: the time scales and the `trex` defaults come from it). Nothing is kept between calls but the time of the open WebM cluster (`base`).
  - WebM: the EBML header to the end of `Tracks` is the initialization segment; `Segment` and `Cluster` are entered (also with unknown size), `SimpleBlock` and `BlockGroup` are frames once whole (a block before its cluster's `Timecode`, or any other element of unknown size, is a bad stream), everything else is skipped once whole. ISO BMFF: `ftyp` (if any) to the end of `moov`; a `moof` with the `mdat` after it is a media segment (`tfhd`, `tfdt`, `trun` with data offsets from the `moof`; `base-data-offset` is refused, as Chromium does), other boxes are skipped.
  - `Track` has the number (the MP4 track id from `tkhd`), the time scale and the default frame duration (`DefaultDuration`).
  - Verify: `aye test browser --module media`: 9 passed. A WebM stream (an initialization segment, a cluster of known size with two blocks, one of unknown size) and a fragmented MP4 (`ftyp`, `moov` with `mvex`, `styp`, `moof`, `mdat`) are each appended in two parts cut at every byte: always one initialization segment and the same frames (track, time, size, key, first byte); the two bad streams are refused.
  - Left of this slice: Matroska lacing, `EncryptedBlock` and content encodings, `BlockGroup` reference times beyond "has one", MP4 `sidx`/`emsg` contents (skipped), `trun` without a data offset, several `trun`s whose data is not contiguous by their own offsets (they are read by offset, so they work), edit lists, 64-bit sizes beyond 1 TiB.
