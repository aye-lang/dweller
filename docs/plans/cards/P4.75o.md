  `page_load.py check --site mistral` with a fast build: pass, 26.2 s wall (before this card's change) and 28.0 s (after; load 2.4, the dump path is unchanged).
- root cause 1, the 280 s: the binary was a debug build. `aye build browser -o target/dweller` defaults to `--mode debug` (`-O0`); `browser/build.sh` builds `--mode fast`. P4.75m's numbers are the debug build's (157 s and 123 s against 162.6 s and 127.0 s here). Every bucket is 9–13× slower at `-O0`; the VM's dispatch loop loses most (bytecode 9.4×), so only there is the VM slower than the tree walker (312 s against 274 s). With `--mode fast` the VM and the tree walker are equal (27.2 s, 27.4 s), and `check` passes in 26 s, under the capture's 30 s CPU. Nothing in the VM is wrong: 19,019 monomorphic ICs against 242 megamorphic, no function on the tree walker.
- root cause 2, the 10,510 tasks: animation frame callbacks of a cross-origin iframe far below the viewport. On the virtual clock `requestAnimationFrame` is a 16 ms timer per callback (`prelude.aye`), and the iframe runs about six rAF loops (its `showcase.js` calls `requestAnimationFrame` 30 times; its `setTimeout`s are 90 ms or more), about 30,000 / 16 × 6 callbacks in `dump`'s 30 s, at 1.4 ms each (fast). `runUntilIdle` stops at `MAX_TASKS` (10,000) in one call, so the page and its frames share that cap. No timer is clamped wrong for this page; but `setTimeout` has no nesting clamp (HTML: from nesting level 5 a timeout is at least 4 ms), a gap for another card. Chrome does not run them: Blink throttles the rendering of a cross-origin frame that is outside the viewport, and a throttled document gets no animation frame callbacks.
- fix: the window does the same. Before each animation frame of the tab's page, `throttleFrames` marks the script of every frame that is cross-origin to the tab's page and whose frame rect does not meet its parent's viewport (scroll offset and size) `throttled`; `frameCallback` returns none for it. A frame not laid out yet is not throttled. When the frame is scrolled into view its callbacks run again. `dump` and the agent keep running them: tried there too, it cut mistral from 27.2 s to 14.5 s (iframe 10,510 → 1,113 tasks, `canvasDraw` 12.2 → 5.1 s) but the agent view lost the ctf-speedrun widget's numbers ("Kimi K3 14" → "Kimi K3 0", "Solved 1 5 of 19" → "Solved 1 of 19"): `dump` never scrolls the frames into view, and the frames' IntersectionObservers check against the frame's own viewport, so their count-ups started at once before.
- files: `browser/engine/throttle-frames.aye` (new), `browser/engine/frame-callback.aye`, `browser/engine/run-animation-frames.aye`, `browser/engine/script.aye` (the `throttled` field), `browser/tests/ui/offscreen-frame-raf.html`, `tests/browser.rs` (`window_offscreen_frame_raf`: no callbacks of a sandboxed srcdoc frame 3000 px down; callbacks after `key:end`; fails without the fix)
- blink: `third_party/blink/renderer/core/frame/local_frame_view.cc` (`CanThrottleRendering`, `UpdateRenderThrottlingStatus`), `core/page/page_animator.cc` (`ServiceScriptedAnimations` skips throttled documents)
