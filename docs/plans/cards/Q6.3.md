## Slice 1: the `flex` and `flex-flow` shorthands, `-webkit-` aliases

- design:
  - `Declarer.flex` (`css/values.aye`): `none | [ <flex-grow> <flex-shrink>? || <flex-basis> ]`. The basis may be first or last; the numbers are one or two and adjacent; three numbers are valid only when the third is a literal `0` (the basis `0px`). An omitted basis is `0%`, as Chromium and Firefox compute and as `parsing/flex-computed.html` expects (css-flexbox-1 now says `0px`; the tests and both engines keep `0%`).
  - `Declarer.flexFlow`: a second direction or a second wrap makes the declaration invalid.
  - `specifiedFlex` (new): the style attribute's `flex` is checked and written as `grow shrink basis`; `flex-flow` as the shortest form (`writeFlexFlowText`: `row` and `nowrap` are dropped unless nothing is left).
  - `checksValue` takes `flex-basis` (so `none`, `-1px`, two values are refused); `validatesInline` takes `flex-grow` and `flex-shrink` (`.0` is written `0`).
  - `expandShorthands` (new, `engine/`): removing a longhand from the style attribute first replaces a shorthand declaration that covers it by its longhands (cssom `removeProperty`). It is general: `css/css-grid`, `css/backgrounds-borders` and `css/css-animations` gain from it too.
  - `css.unprefixed` lists the twelve flexbox names (`-webkit-flex`, `-webkit-align-items`, `-webkit-order`, ...), for style sheets and, through `styleValue` and `setStyleDecl`, for the style attribute.
- references: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox-1/#flex-property , https://drafts.csswg.org/cssom/#dom-cssstyledeclaration-removeproperty , https://compat.spec.whatwg.org/#css-simple-aliases . No Chromium file read.
- files: `browser/css/values.aye`, `layers.aye`, `checks-value.aye`, `specified-longhand.aye`, `specified-shorthand.aye`, new `specified-flex.aye`, `write-flex-flow-text.aye`; `browser/engine/dom_api.aye`, new `expand-shorthands.aye`, `append-longhands.aye`; new fixture `browser/tests/jsdom/style_flex_shorthand.html` and `.tree`; `browser/tests/style/flex_props.style` blessed, one line: `flex: 1` computes `flex-basis: 0%` (was `0px`), the value the browsers give; `browser/tools/conformance/css_css-flexbox.json`.

## Slice 2: images with an aspect ratio as flex items

- cause: the flex base size and the cross size of an image ignored its aspect ratio. The basis was the max-content width (the natural width), whatever the definite cross size or the min/max sizes in the cross axis said.
- design (css-flexbox-1 9.2 step 3 B, 9.4 step 7):
  - `stretchedRatioBasis`: a stretched image in a single-line row with a definite height takes its basis from that cross size (within `min-height`/`max-height`) through the ratio.
  - `imageBasis`: any other image with `width: auto` and `flex-basis: auto` takes the width of `replacedDims`, or the width its clamped height gives through the ratio. `min-width` and `max-width` do not enter the basis (they clamp the hypothetical size).
  - `ratioHeight` (rows, in `itemContents`) and `ratioWidth` (columns, after the main size is set, when the item is not stretched): the cross size follows the flexed main size through the ratio.
  - `naturalRatio`: `replacedRatio`, but an SVG image without `viewBox` and without both `width` and `height` has no ratio (its 300x150 fallback is not one).
- references: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox-1/#algo-main-item , https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox-1/#algo-cross-item , https://drafts.csswg.org/css-sizing-4/#preferred-aspect-ratio . No Chromium file read.
- files: `browser/layout/flex.aye` (four wiring lines), new `stretched-ratio-basis.aye`, `image-basis.aye`, `ratio-height.aye`, `ratio-width.aye`, `natural-ratio.aye`; new fixture `browser/tests/layout/flex_image_ratio.html` and `.layout` (golden new; the seven sizes read against the WPT expectations: 40x40, 10x10, 34x34, 30x30, 40x40, 100x100, 150x150).

## Slice 3: definite sizes of stretched items, auto margins across

- cause: an item was laid out again at its stretched height only when that height differed from its content's, so a percentage height of its child stayed `auto` when both were equal (`percentage-heights-001.html`: `height: 50%` gave 50, not 25). An item with an `auto` margin in the cross axis was stretched, and the margin did nothing.
- design:
  - `relayoutAt` takes `definite`: a stretched item is definite (css-flexbox-1 9.8, rule 3), a flexed column item only when the container's height is. A definite item with a child whose `height`, `min-height`, `max-height` or `flex-basis` is a percentage is laid out again even at the same height.
  - `crossAutoOffset` (new): `auto` margins in the cross axis take the free space of the line (both: centred; start only: pushed to the end) and the item is not stretched (8.1).
- references: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox-1/#definite-sizes , https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox-1/#auto-margins . No Chromium file read.
- files: `browser/layout/flex.aye`, new `cross-auto-offset.aye`, `has-percent-child.aye`; new fixture `browser/tests/layout/flex_definite_percent.html` and `.layout` (golden new, six boxes read: 25 high half, 0 high percentage item in an indefinite column, centred and bottom positions).

## Slice 4: the baselines of a flex container

- cause: a flex container took its baselines as a block does, from its first and last child in document order that has one (`childBaselines`). `row-reverse`, `column-reverse`, `wrap-reverse`, `order`, and an item with `align-self: baseline` further along the line were ignored, and an item without a baseline did not give its synthesized one.
- design (css-flexbox-1 8.5): `flexBaselines` runs at the end of `layoutFlex`, where the lines are known. The first baseline comes from the line that starts first across, the last one from the line that starts last. In that line (`baselineItem`): an item that takes part in baseline alignment (`baseline` for the first, `last baseline` for the last, rows only), else the item at the inline start (first) or inline end (last) of the line by position, with its synthesized baseline when it has none. A flex container that scrolls or clips and has a definite height keeps its baselines inside its border box. `rebaseline` (incremental layout) gives up at a flex container, so a full layout computes them.
- references: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox-1/#flex-baselines , https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align-3/#synthesize-baseline . No Chromium file read; "startmost by position" is what `alignment/flex-align-baseline-flex-001.html` expects for `row-reverse` (the spec's text reads as order).
- files: `browser/layout/flex.aye` (one call), `block.aye` (the flex arm no longer calls `childBaselines`), `incremental.aye`, new `flex-baselines.aye`, `baseline-item.aye`, `main-start.aye`; new fixture `browser/tests/layout/flex_container_baselines.html` and `.layout` (golden new, five positions read).
- verify (2026-11-16): `wpt.py run css/css-flexbox -j 3`: 3928 -> 3954/5816 (68.0%), no file below the slice 3 baseline (`flex-align-baseline-flex-001` 32 -> 48/48, six `flexbox-baseline-*` reftests). `cargo test --release --test browser`: 249 passed. `check_taste.py`, `check_layers.py`, `gen_property_ids.py --check`, `aye check browser`: 0.

## Slice 5: column containers: wrapping at `max-height`, the cross axis in rtl

- causes, two, both in column containers:
  - a column wrapped only when its height was definite. With `height: auto` and a `max-height`, the lines must break at the `max-height` (`align-content-vert-001b.html`: 55 subtests).
  - `direction: rtl` did not turn the cross axis of a column: the cross start is the right edge (`flexbox_justifycontent-rtl-002.html`: 12, and the `flexbox_rtl-*` reftests).

## Slice 6: `flex-wrap: balance`, integer properties in transitions

- cause: `flex-wrap` took one keyword, so `balance` (css-flexbox-2) dropped the declaration and the container did not wrap at all. A transition of `order` showed fractions (`2.6`).
- design:
  - `flexWrapValue`: `nowrap | [ wrap | wrap-reverse ] || balance`, computed as `balance` or `wrap-reverse balance` (`wrap` is implied). `Declarer.flexFlow` takes up to three components: the direction first or last, the rest is the wrap value. The style attribute writes `flex-wrap` in canonical form.
  - `breakLines` (the line breaking of `layoutFlex`, moved out) and `balanceLines`: with `balance`, the lines are broken at the smallest limit that keeps the line count of the plain greedy breaking (a bisection of the limit, 24 steps), so the lines come out as even as that count allows. `wrapsReverse` replaces the four `wrap-reverse` keyword tests.
  - `clampToRange` rounds the value of an integer property (`order`, `z-index`) to the nearest whole number.
  - an experiment, not kept: `allowsDiscrete` for every property (the form before 5f00c74). `animation/discrete-no-interpolation.html` did not gain and `css/css-animations` lost 26 (`display-interpolation`, `transition-properties-not-animatable`).
- references: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox-2/#flex-wrap-property , https://drafts.csswg.org/css-values-4/#combine-integers . No Chromium file read.
- files: `browser/css/values.aye` (`flexFlow`, one arm of `parseValue`), `specified-longhand.aye`, new `flex-wrap-value.aye`; `browser/layout/flex.aye`, `align-lines.aye`, `mirror-across.aye`, `flex-static-shift.aye`, new `break-lines.aye`, `balance-lines.aye`, `wraps-reverse.aye`; `browser/engine/scroll-origin.aye` (one line); `browser/animation/clamp-to-range.aye`; new fixture `browser/tests/layout/flex_wrap_balance.html` and `.layout` (golden new: 3+1 without balance, 2+2 with it, and the reversed form).
