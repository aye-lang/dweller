# Q6.7: WPT `css/values-color` to its floor

- goal: the area (`css/css-values`, `css/css-color`) reaches its floor of 70 (57.9 in the table; 13463/23409, 57.5%, measured at the start of this card). One cause per cluster, largest first. The 2 points of room are not reached: see "why it stops".
- design (clusters in the order done; subtests are of the area's 23409):
  - the inline setter kept any value with a `(` as written. A value with a math function is now parsed like a sheet declaration (`parsesAs`), its math blocks must evaluate (`css/valid-math-blocks.aye`, `math-well-formed.aye`), and a block of absolute units is written simplified, `calc(2px)` for `clamp(1px, 2px, 3px)`, also inside another function (`uses-math.aye`, `specified-math.aye`, `write-math-aware.aye`, `is-absolute-math.aye`, `write-simplified-math.aye`, `write-calc-value.aye`, `write-calc-unit.aye`). Percentages are simplified only without a comparison. 13463 -> 14006.
  - the evaluator (`calc.aye`) knew `calc`, `min`, `max`, `clamp`, `round`, `mod`, `rem`, `abs`, `sign`. Added `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `asin`, `acos`, `atan`, `atan2`, `pow`, `sqrt`, `hypot`, `log`, `exp`, `progress` (`plain-math.aye`, `trig-value.aye`, `inverse-trig-value.aye`, `hypot-value.aye`, `exponential-value.aye`, `progress-value.aye`), a kind on `Quantity` for angles and times (`angle-q.aye`, `calc-typed.aye`; `rotate` and the time grammar read them: `rotate-angle.aye`, `time-seconds.aye`), and division of two quantities of one type. 14006 -> 15246 with the next item.
  - relative colours serialize `rgb`/`hsl` for `rgba`/`hsla`, `currentcolor` in lower case and `xyz-d65` for `xyz` (`write-color-function-name.aye`, `write-unresolved-relative.aye`, `write-relative-rest.aye`).
  - missing components (`none`) carry to the analogous component of the target space (`carry-missing.aye`, `component-kind.aye`); a mix in `hsl`/`hwb` with a missing component stays in that space; the `hwb` hue is powerless when whiteness and blackness reach 100%. 15246 -> 15457, then 16182 -> 16293.
  - `color-mix()` with one colour, with percentages that sum to 0, without the default `shorter hue` and `in oklab` in its serialization (`color-mix.aye`, `mix-colors.aye`, `write-single-mix.aye`, `write-mix-method.aye`, `without-shorter-hue.aye`). 15457 -> 15794.
  - the `display-p3-linear` space (index 16 of `COLOR_SPACES`; `color-space-kinds.aye` names the two range tests that were `<= 8` and `>= 13`). 15794 -> 16088.
  - `lab()`, `lch()`, `oklab()`, `oklch()` and `color()` keep a `calc()` per channel in their specified form (`has-calc-channel.aye`, `write-calc-channels.aye`). 16088 -> 16182.
  - `object-position` (appended to `PROPERTIES`, id 191, `PKind.Layers`, the position grammar of `background-position`), computed as two length-percentages (`write-computed-position.aye`, `write-len-pct.aye`). 16293 -> 16314.
  - legacy `rgb()`/`hsl()` with commas take math channels (`legacy-function.aye`, `math-token.aye`), a channel at exactly one half rounds up (`hwb(120 30% 50%)` is `rgb(77, 128, 77)`), and legacy colours keep their unrounded channels for conversion (`legacy-channels.aye`). 16314 -> 16405, then the serialization items above to 16508.
- decisions taken without asking (plan defaults):
  - an unknown colour function is still kept as written by the inline setter. Rejecting it gained 110 subtests (`color-invalid-color-layers-function`, `-contrast-color-`) and lost 180 that pass only by echoing (`color-valid-color-layers-function` 144, `contrast-color` 15, `alpha()` 22); those are passes of text, not of a feature.
  - math uses `std.math` (the platform libm), as the colour code already does; the practice about libm is written for `Math` in the JS engine.
- why it stops at the floor: the next clusters are features, not causes: `calc-size()` and `interpolate-size` (3300 subtests), `if()` (240), `random()` (about 450), typed `attr()` (95), `calc-mix()` (170). The floor plus 2 needs 450 more subtests, which is one of them.
