#### P4.75t: mistral's 7 s of forced layout

- goal: cut mistral's `script_layout` (7160 ms over 8123 forced layouts, P4.75r) by finding the dominant cost of one forced layout and doing what Blink does for it; identical output.
- measurements (mistral, P4.75d archive, `browser/build.sh` fast binary of `origin/p4-75r-restyle-speed`, M2 Pro, capped; one `dump --md --js` replay with `DWELLER_TIMING=1 DWELLER_JS_NATIVES=1 DWELLER_LAYOUT_WHY=1`, and a macOS `sample` of 24 s of the run):
  - forced layouts by frame: the `visual-intelligence` iframe 6926 (3779 ms), the page 881 (2659 ms), `ctf-speedrun` 230 (728 ms), `malware-triage` 82 (110 ms). Nearly all are forced by `metric` (the `offsetWidth`/`offsetHeight`/`clientWidth` family), then `offsetParent` (239) and `rects` (`getBoundingClientRect`, 32).
  - what each does: 7522 partial restyles (`restyle`, 2947 ms), 1584 full layouts (`layout_full`, 2153 ms: the page 464 at 3.7 ms, the iframe 1009), 1055 full restyles (`style_full`, 739 ms), 1778 incremental relayouts (`layout_dirty`, 157 ms).
  - full instead of incremental: `relayoutDirty` refused 1294 times; new `DWELLER_LAYOUT_WHY` reasons in it say why: `relayout:floats` 420 (all of the page's: its tree has floats), `relayout:queried` 850 (the iframe: container queries in a non-shared layout), `dropped` 10, `shadows` 9, `not_element` 4; none for `no_box`, `rebuild`, `relayout`.
  - the profile of `geometry` (2836 samples): `font.genericOf` 617 self (22%), `sheetSourceKey` 583–621 (21%; `treeSourceKey` self 499), partial restyles (`restyleSubtrees`) 839, layout (`layOut`) 1134, of it `layoutFlex` 907 (the flex items' contents: 864, no measuring twice of note), `layoutInline` 783, `alignLine` 484, `textMetrics` 467 (of it `genericOf`), `intrinsic` 81.
- cause:
  1. `fontOf` classifies the `font-family` list of the style (`genericOf`: split the list at commas and compare each family case-insensitively with ~80 known names, three lists) for every text piece, line and metric query; mistral's family (`Inter`-like web font names first) misses the lists and walks all of them every time. Blink resolves a style's font once (`FontDescription` → `FontFallbackList` on the `ComputedStyle`) and keeps fonts by family in `FontCache`.
  2. `sheetSourceKey` (walk the whole document, hash every style element's text byte by byte) ran three times in one forced layout: `takeSheetChanges` in `geometry`, again in `computedStyling` through `layoutStyling`, and once more there for `computed_sheets`, with no DOM change between them. Blink works out the active style sheets once per style update (`StyleEngine::UpdateActiveStyle`), and only for tree scopes marked dirty.
- design (algorithm only, no code copied):
  - `font.genericCached`: an 8-entry cache of family lists (≤ 96 bytes, copied) to their `Generic`, round robin; `fontOf` uses it. The canvas keeps `genericOf`.
  - `scriptSheetKey`: while `geometry` runs (`holdSheetKey(s, true)`, and `defer holdSheetKey(s, false)`), the first `sheetSourceKey` of the script's page is kept in `Script.sheet_key_now` and returned to the later calls; outside `geometry` (`getComputedStyle`) it is computed as before. Nothing in `geometry` changes the DOM (shadow roots come only from the parser and `attachShadow`).
  - the reasons of `relayoutDirty`'s refusals are logged with `DWELLER_LAYOUT_WHY` (`relayout:<why>`).
- files: new `browser/font/generic-cached.aye`, `browser/engine/script-sheet-key.aye`, `browser/engine/hold-sheet-key.aye`; wiring in `browser/layout/values.aye` (`fontOf`), `browser/engine/script.aye` (`sheet_key_held`, `sheet_key_now`), `browser/engine/geometry.aye` (hold, `layoutStyling`, the `relayout:` reasons), `browser/engine/take-sheet-changes.aye`, `browser/engine/window_api.aye` (`computedStyling`).
- blink: `platform/fonts/font_cache.cc` (`GetFontPlatformData`), `platform/fonts/font_fallback_list.cc`; `core/css/style_engine.cc` (`SetNeedsActiveStyleUpdate`, `MarkTreeScopeDirty`, `UpdateActiveStyle`).
