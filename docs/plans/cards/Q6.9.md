# Q6.9: WPT `css/floats-tables` to its floor

- goal: the area (`css/CSS2/floats`, `css/CSS2/floats-clear`, `css/css-tables`) reaches its floor of 65, target 68 (55.5 in the table, 888/1601; 878/1601, 54.8%, measured in this container at the start of this card). Taken from lane css-layout by the integrator's message. One cause per cluster, largest first.
- design (clusters in the order done; subtests are of the area's 1601):
  - a declaration whose name is longer than 32 bytes was dropped (`-webkit-border-horizontal-spacing` is 33): the name buffer of `addDeclaration` is 48 bytes. `getComputedStyle().borderSpacing` reads the running transition (`engine/write-current-spacing.aye`).
  - `caption-side`, `empty-cells`, `table-layout` did not exist: appended to `PROPERTIES` as keyword properties (ids 210, 211, 212). A keyword property transitions discretely under `allow-discrete` (`animation/allows-discrete.aye`), and its keyword is kept in the snapshot (`snapshot-value.aye`). With the item above: `css-tables/animations` +106, `parsing` +14, `inheritance` +6.
  - a float inside a table cell did not count in the cell's content extent (`layout/content-extent.aye`).
  - a block that starts a formatting context was placed beside the floats at its top edge only. It is now laid out again until it fits: narrower when a float lower down intrudes along its height, and below the next float bottom when its border box does not fit the band (`layout/block.aye`, the loop after `layoutBlockLevel`, at most 8 rounds). A negative margin on the side of a float does not pull the box into the float (`layout/float-side-pull.aye`). `CSS2/floats` +33, `floats-clear` +11.
  - `border-spacing` is validated by the inline setter (`css/specified-shorthand.aye`): `parsing` +4.
  - the table attributes `rules` and `frame`, and `border` with a value that is not a number, had no presentational hints (`css/table-border-hint.aye`, `cell-border-hint.aye`, `table-rules.aye`, `frame-styles.aye`, `table-border-pixels.aye`); the UA sheet has `table, td, th { border-color: gray }`. `html-to-css-mapping-2` 14/25 -> 24/25.
  - a table higher than its rows, or a row group with a `height`, kept its rows at their content height. The extra height is now shared (`layout/stretched-table.aye`, `stretch-table.aye`, `stretch-rows.aye`, `stretch-track.aye`, `share-height.aye`, `grow-tracks.aye`, `track-mark.aye`, `has-fixed-height.aye`, `prefer-bodies.aye`, `grow-row.aye`, `place-table-rows.aye`, `place-group-rows.aye`): first to tracks with a percentage height up to their share, then to tracks without a height in proportion to their heights (equally when all are empty), then to tracks with a fixed height, then to all. Among the row groups of a table the bodies take it when there is one; inside a group its rows share it the same way. Cells are stretched and their contents shifted by `vertical-align`. A row of a table with no columns is as wide as the table's content box. `css-tables/tentative` +51 (`table-height-redistribution` 5/31 -> 27/31, `table-rows-with-zero-columns` 0/12 -> 12/12, `tbody-height-redistribution` 2/12 -> 9/12).

  - the three new properties are parsed, cascaded, inherited, serialized and animated; layout does not read them (Left).
  - the gray default border colour of `table, td, th` changes pages that set `border-style` and `border-width` without a colour on a cell; browsers draw those gray. Four style goldens were blessed after reading the diff (`browser/tests/style/{hints,no_quirks,quirks,ua_defaults}.style`): the only change is 44 added lines, `border-*-color: rgb(128, 128, 128)` on tables and cells, which is the UA rule of the HTML rendering section.
