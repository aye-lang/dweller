# P10.52: css/overflow toward its threshold

- goal: WPT area `css/overflow` (`css/css-overflow`) from 28.5% toward its 60% threshold, without changing the window's real scrolling (ui/ scroll frames, wheel and trackpad momentum).
- cause groups (failing subtests at start, 1364 of 1907; this run measured 543/1907, not the 482/1888 of the planned table, because the checkout is newer): line-clamp, block-ellipsis, `continue`, `max-lines` reftests and parsing (~280, not started); scrollable overflow geometry (`scrollWidth`/`scrollHeight`: transforms, the in-flow bounds with their margins plus padding, the scroll origin; ~160); discrete interpolation of unsupported properties (`overflow-no-interpolation` 66, `scroll-axis-lock-interpolation` 42); scroll markers and `::scroll-button()` selectors (~120, not started); `overflow` shorthand and longhands parse, serialize and compute (~60); `overflow-clip-margin` (~60 with its reftests, not started); `scrollbar-gutter` (~55, not started); `text-overflow` and `text-overflow-string` reftests (~45, not started).
- design: script geometry (`engine/geometry.aye` `scrollWidth`, `scrollHeight`, element `scrollTo` clamping) uses `scrollableOverflow`: the padding box, the border boxes of the descendants (not into boxes that clip, not fixed ones) as their transform maps them, and the in-flow children's margin boxes at their static positions (no relative offset) grown by the padding; overflow before the scroll origin (left and top; right for `rtl`, flipped by flex `*-reverse` and `wrap-reverse`) is unreachable and dropped. Block layout does not mirror `rtl` content yet, so with a right origin the area reaches whichever side is farther. `overflow-x/y: clip` axes cannot be scrolled by script. `clip` no longer computes to `hidden` when the other axis scrolls (only `visible` becomes `auto`). `getComputedStyle().overflow` serializes the shorthand; inline `overflow`, `overflow-x`, `overflow-y` validate their keywords and collapse `x x` to `x`. The document's scrolling area (`documentArea`) and ui/ are unchanged.
- files: `browser/engine/scrollable-overflow.aye`, `add-overflow.aye`, `inflow-bounds.aye`, `scroll-origin.aye`, `geometry.aye`, `window_api.aye`, `prelude.aye`; `browser/css/write-overflow.aye`, `specified-overflow.aye`, `specified-value.aye`, `cascade.aye`.
- reference: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-overflow-3/#scrollable, https://drafts.csswg.org/cssom-view/; chromium `third_party/blink/renderer/core/layout/scrollable_overflow_calculator.cc` (inflow bounds, unreachable region), algorithm only.
- verify: `conformance.py run css/overflow` 543 → 670 of 1907 (28.5% → 35.1%); `conformance.py check` → 0; `gen_property_ids.py --check`, `check_taste.py`, `check_layers.py` → 0; `cargo test --release --test browser -- style_fixtures layout_fixtures jsdom_fixtures errors` → 0; `-- window_ scroll` → 54 passed, only `window_webgl` failed (no ANGLE here). No golden changed.
- status: partial 2026-10-17, 35.1% (threshold 60%), stopped at the time budget.
