# Q2.15p: `overscroll-behavior` across frames

- goal: last item of the lane's second list [Q2.10 Left: every wheel event's rest bubbles from a child; Chromium "honors `overscroll-behavior` across frames"]. A cross-site frame whose document says `overscroll-behavior: contain` or `none` still passed the scroll it could not use to the page around it. (Ids m, n and o of Q2.15 were taken by other lanes; this card is p.)
- design: the child's renderer computes what a wheel (or a bubbled scroll from its own child) left unused (`scrollChain`). For a renderer that is embedded, `keptAtBoundary` now drops an axis of that rest when the document's root element has `overscroll-behavior` other than `auto` on it (`engine.viewportChainStops`, the same `chainStops` that stops chaining at an element inside a document). Nothing is sent as `wheel_unused` then, so the embedder does not scroll. No message changes; the browser's check of `wheel_unused` is as before.
- spec read: https://drafts.csswg.org/css-overscroll-1/#overscroll-behavior-properties (the root element's value applies to the viewport; `contain` and `none` stop scroll chaining to the parent scroller, which for a frame is its embedder). Chromium: `content/browser/renderer_host/render_widget_host_view_child_frame.cc` (the child bubbles a scroll only when its renderer reports it unconsumed and chainable), `cc/input/scroll_state.cc`.
- files: new `browser/engine/viewport-chain-stops.aye`, `browser/renderer/kept-at-boundary.aye`; `browser/renderer/scroll-chain.aye`; `browser/tests/ui/oopif-input-child.html` (`?contain`); `tests/browser/window_renderer_limits.rs`.
- verify (2026-11-19, Linux x86_64): `window_renderer_limits_overscroll`, with `DWELLER_PROCESS=site`, three wheels over the frame of `oopif-input.html`: without the property the frame ends at its scroll end (`scroll 220`) and the page has scrolled (the frame's y is under 36); with `?contain` the frame ends at `scroll 220` and stays at y 36. `window_renderer_oopif_input_wheel` (Q2.10) passes unchanged.
