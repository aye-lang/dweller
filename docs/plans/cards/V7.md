# V7: TUI backend

The card is in `docs/plans/vision.md`.

## Status: done as one rendered frame, 2026-11-16 (the interactive loop is Left)

Done note:
- `browser/tui/` (new platform module): `CellMeasurer`, a
  `font.Measurer` where every narrow character is 1 wide and a line is 1
  high (wide characters 2, combining marks 0), and a display-list
  consumer: `drawList` puts `Fill`, `Quad` (its bounds), `Text`, `Clip`
  and `Unclip` items into a grid of cells (code point, foreground,
  background, bold), and `writeAnsi` writes the grid as one frame (cursor
  home, a truecolor SGR where the look changes, rows joined by CR LF).
- The unit is 1 CSS px = 1 cell. The app's terminal stylesheet sets
  `font-size: 1px` on the root, so `em` is a cell too, and lengths are
  cells: `margin: 1px 2px` is one row and two columns
  (`examples/native/counter/counter.tui.css`). `css/`, `layout/` and
  `paint/` are unchanged: the same cascade, block and inline layout and
  display list as the window's.
- `APP --tui [--width COLUMNS] [--height ROWS] [--events WORDS]` in the
  shell (`run-tui.aye`, `show-tui.aye`): builds the app, runs the event
  words (`click:COLUMN,ROW`, `text:`, `key:`, `wheel:`), prints the frame.
  `shell.runLook` takes the window's and the terminal's stylesheet
  (`shell.run` is kept).
- So the V3 component (`counter/widget`) renders in a terminal with no
  change to it, and without V6: the document is still a `dom.Document`.
- Verify: `native::native_counter_tui`: two frames of the counter at
  40 x 12 (after two clicks on `+1` and typed text; after `Add` and a
  scroll to the end) are fed to the VT parser of V4 (`term dump`) and
  compared with `expected/tui.grid` and `tui-added.grid`, read before
  blessing: the count is 2, the field shows the typed text, the buttons
  have their background, the last row is the added one with its
  highlight. Whole suite on the merged tree, cloud machine, proxy
  variables unset: 271 passed, 0 failed (`target/v7-merged.log`); `check_taste.py`, `check_layers.py`, `gen_property_ids.py --check`, `aye check browser`: 0.

Left:
- An interactive loop: the terminal in raw mode on the alternate screen,
  keys and SGR mouse reports as the shell's events, a frame after each
  change (only the rows that changed), resize on SIGWINCH. The shell's
  event handling and V4's key table are the two halves.
- Borders as box-drawing characters (a border is a filled cell now),
  underline and italic, the cursor in a focused field, 256-color and
  16-color output for terminals without truecolor.
- Images, shadows, opacity layers, filters and SVG items are skipped.
- The UA sheet's lengths are for pixels (a button's 2 px border, a
  list's 40 px indent): a terminal UA sheet instead of per-app resets.
- Sub-cell positions are rounded per item; two boxes that meet at a half
  cell can overlap or leave a gap.
