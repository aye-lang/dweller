# P10.58: wheel, trackpad and keys scroll the scroll container under the pointer

- goal: an element with `overflow: auto/scroll` (chat panes, sidebars, code blocks, carousels, modals) scrolls by wheel, trackpad and keys, with scroll chaining, `overscroll-behavior`, trackpad latching, `scroll`/`scrollend` events, at about the cost of a page scroll.
- design: element scroll offsets moved from `Script.scrolls` to `Page.box_scrolls` (`layout.BoxScroll`), so pages without scripts scroll too; every tree of the page points at the map (`Tree.box_scrolls`), and scripts (`geometry.aye`), paint and hit testing read the same offsets. Paint shifts the content of a scroller by its offset (`Builder.sx/sy`, `scroll-into.aye`; layers inside scrollers get the offsets of the scrollers around them in `clippedLayer`); hit testing shifts the point into scrolled content (`hit.aye`). The scroll helpers (`isScroller`, `clipsOverflow`, `maxScroll`, `scrollableOverflow`, `addOverflow`) take a `layout.Tree` instead of the script `Geometry`. The window (`flushWheel`) asks `engine.wheelScrollTarget` for the innermost scroller around the hit node that can scroll in the delta's direction (user-scrollable axes: `auto`, `scroll`), stopping at one whose `overscroll-behavior` is not `auto`; none: the frame, then the page, as before. Precise (fractional) deltas within 150 ms keep the target latched (`wheel-scroll-element.aye`), past its edge too; a gesture that began on the page stays there. `scroll` fires at the element (as a task, through the prelude's `scrolled`), `scrollend` at it (document for the page) 150 ms after the last wheel (`end-wheel-gesture.aye`; non-bubbling at elements). A click remembers the scroller under it (`note-click-scroller.aye`); arrows, PageUp/Down, Space, Home/End then scroll it, else the page (`key-scroll-element.aye`). New longhands `overscroll-behavior-x/-y` (`auto contain none`) and the `overscroll-behavior` shorthand (`axisPair`, shared with `overflow`). `wheel` listeners already could cancel (passive at window/document/body).
